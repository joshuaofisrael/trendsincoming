Kylie Jenner's $50 million Fantasy Bra

Jacob & Co. built it with 4,410 diamonds around a 75.54-carat Fancy Vivid yellow diamond. It took 15 craftspeople more than 1,000 hours, weighs just under four pounds, and at $50 million it's the priciest Fantasy Bra ever, per Vogue.

The quick version

At the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show on October 18, 2026, Kylie Jenner walked in the Fantasy Bra, the first time the brand has put one on its runway since 2018. Made with Jacob & Co., it's valued at $50 million, which Vogue says beats the previous record by about $35 million.

Why Kylie

Victoria's Secret creative chief Adam Selman told Vogue the Fantasy Bra was one of fans' most requested comebacks, and that Jenner was picked as a "cultural powerhouse." Jacob Arabo said the hardest part was making an all-metal piece comfortable, so it was built to her exact measurements with ongoing fittings.

The show also crowned a brand-new Angel, 21-year-old Solange Omon, winner of the YouTube series Angels Among Us. Our Victoria's Secret Fashion Show 2026 page has the recap and replay links.
