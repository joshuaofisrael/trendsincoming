The quick version

Fall beauty is going warm, moody and a little dramatic. Pinterest's first Beauty Trend Report says hair is heading to mocha, copper and cherry cola, ELLE says milky Ghost Rose nails are beating burgundy, and runways from Saint Laurent to Gucci brought back the smoky eye with a burgundy lip.

HairMocha, copper, cherry cola (Pinterest Beauty Trend Report, Oct 6)
NailsGhost Rose: milky blush-nude (ELLE)
MakeupSmoky eyes and burgundy lips (Page Six Style, Galore)
Runway momentVictoria's Secret show, Oct 18

Try it without a big commitment

  • Cherry cola hair: ask for a gloss or semi-permanent color first.
  • Ghost Rose nails: a sheer pink base plus a milky top coat gets you there at home.
  • Smoky eye: start with a brown shadow smudged along the lash line, then add depth.
