The quick version

Tesla beat on profit, but the details were mixed. In its October 21 report, adjusted earnings came in at $0.48 a share versus $0.43 expected and revenue at $28.05 billion, per CNBC. Auto revenue fell 3%, energy fell 16%, and free cash flow dropped 62%. Shares slipped more than 3% after hours.

The SpaceX question

On SpaceX's own call, Elon Musk called a combination of the two companies a "good idea", and both boards have formed special committees, according to CNBC. Nothing has been agreed. If it moves forward, expect a long process with independent reviews.

What to watch

The big swing factors are car demand, the energy business after a down quarter, and how much of that roughly $20 billion spending plan goes to AI and robotaxi work. Tesla's update letter has the details.
