The quick version

The biggest consumer deal this month: Bumble agreed to be acquired for about $1.3 billion in an all-cash deal with Ithaca Holdings, according to its SEC filing on October 13. Meanwhile, a Barclays note suggested a Tesla and SpaceX merger could be announced before Tesla's December annual meeting. That one is speculation, and nothing has been announced.

How to read a deal headline

"Agreed to be acquired" means a signed deal that still needs approvals. "In talks" or "exploring" means it may never happen. An analyst note, like the Barclays Tesla call, is an opinion, not a filing. The company's own SEC filing is the source to trust.

Quick answers

Who is buying Bumble?

Ithaca Holdings, in an all-cash deal valued at about $1.3 billion, per Bumble's SEC filing on October 13, 2026.

Are Tesla and SpaceX merging?

Nothing has been announced. A Barclays note said a merger could be announced before Tesla's December annual meeting.

Where can I read merger filings?

On SEC EDGAR. Public companies usually file an 8-K within four business days of signing a deal.
