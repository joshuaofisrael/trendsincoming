The quick version

Crystal Lake is Peacock's Friday the 13th prequel series, and all eight episodes are streaming now (they dropped together on October 15, 2026). Linda Cardellini plays Pam Voorhees, the grieving mom behind the original 1980 film's twist, in a story that starts in 1972 at Camp Crystal Lake, New Jersey.

What it's about (no spoilers)

Jason drowns at camp, his body is never found, and nine months later Pam is still not okay. When body parts start turning up in the woods, the police chief's journalist sister starts pulling on a conspiracy thread, and Pam's own past turns out to be a lot darker than the town's lunch lady should have. Variety calls it more psychological thriller than straight slasher, with plenty of gore.

The episode titles are a love letter

Fans clocked this fast: episodes include "A New Beginning," "The New Blood," "Friday..." and "The Final Chapter," which echo the titles of Friday the 13th sequels. Variety singles out Episode 7, "Friday...," a 1959-set flashback, as the best of the bunch.

What critics and fans say

The split is pretty clean. Critics love Cardellini and the craft, and some find the middle episodes slow and the conspiracy overstuffed. The Guardian gave it 2 out of 5; Peter Travers gave it 3 out of 4. Audience scores are running higher than critics.

Quick answers

Where can I watch Crystal Lake?

Only on Peacock. All eight episodes premiered on October 15, 2026.

How many episodes are in Crystal Lake season 1?

Eight, all released at once.

Who plays Pamela Voorhees in Crystal Lake?

Linda Cardellini. Betsy Palmer played the role in the original 1980 Friday the 13th.

Is Jason in Crystal Lake?

Yes, as a child. Callum Vinson plays young Jason Voorhees.

Do I need to see Friday the 13th before Crystal Lake?

No. It's a prequel set years before the 1980 film, but fans of the movies will catch a lot of callbacks.

Is Crystal Lake on Netflix or Hulu?

No. It's a Peacock original.
