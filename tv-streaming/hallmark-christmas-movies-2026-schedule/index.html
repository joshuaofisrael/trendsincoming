Quick answers

How many new Hallmark Christmas movies are there in 2026?

Hallmark Channel's official Countdown to Christmas 2026 preview lists 34 new original movies, plus the Season 3 premiere of the series Mistletoe Murders and the special The Hallmark Christmas Experience: A Hometown Holiday.

When does Countdown to Christmas 2026 start and end?

The first premiere, 'Tis the Season for Setups, aired Friday, October 16, 2026. The last new movie, Save the Date for Christmas, premieres Sunday, December 20, 2026 at 8/7c.

What time do new Hallmark Christmas movies air?

Hallmark lists premieres every Friday, Saturday and Sunday at 8/7c. Thanksgiving weekend adds a second premiere at 6/5c on Friday Nov 27, Saturday Nov 28 and Sunday Nov 29, and Christmas Delivered premieres on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday Nov 26, at 8/7c.

What Hallmark movie is on tonight?

No new Countdown to Christmas premiere tonight. The next one is Who’s Coming for Christmas? on Fri Oct 30 at 8/7c.

When is the Mistletoe Murders season 3 premiere?

Thursday, November 5, 2026 at 8/7c on Hallmark Channel, starring Sarah Drew and Peter Mooney, with guest stars Catherine Bell and Holly Robinson Peete.

When is The Hallmark Christmas Experience special?

The Hallmark Christmas Experience: A Hometown Holiday airs Saturday, December 5, 2026 at 10/9c, hosted by Jonathan Bennett and Melissa Peterman, right after the A Grand Biltmore Christmas premiere.

Where can I stream Hallmark Christmas movies?

Hallmark's own streaming service is Hallmark+. Hallmark's preview page does not list next-day streaming dates for each movie, so check the Hallmark+ app for a title before you plan around it. Live airings are on Hallmark Channel.

Is there a Hanukkah movie in Countdown to Christmas 2026?

Yes. Eight Nights for Love, starring Hunter King and Dan Jeannotte, premieres Sunday, November 29, 2026 at 6/5c.
