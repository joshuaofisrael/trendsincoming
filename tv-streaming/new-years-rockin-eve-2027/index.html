New Year's Rockin' Eve 2027: Hosts, Performers and How to Watch

By The Trends Incoming team · Checked Oct 27, 2026 against the Dick Clark Productions announcement

Short answer: Dick Clark's New Year's Rockin' Eve with Ryan Seacrest 2027 airs live on Thursday, December 31, 2026, from 8 p.m. ET on ABC, and for the first time ever it also streams on Disney+. The ball drops at midnight Eastern, which is 11 p.m. Central and 9 p.m. Pacific.

How to watch the ball drop 2027

OptionWhat we know
ABCLive from 8 p.m. ET (8/7c) on Thu Dec 31. Check your local ABC station for start times outside Eastern and Central.
Disney+Simulcast alongside ABC, a first in the show's history. You need a Disney+ subscription.
Times Square Alliance livestreamLast year the Alliance ran a free, commercial free webcast on TimesSquareNYC.org from 6 p.m., plus an accessible stream with open captions and ASL. We'll confirm the 2027 stream when the Alliance posts it.
Other networksCNN, NBC and Fox have not posted 2027 New Year's Eve plans that we could verify. We'll add them once they are officially announced.

Last year's numbers

The 2026 edition reached 46 million total viewers, the No. 1 program of the night, and more than 30 million watched the ball drop at midnight, according to Dick Clark Productions citing Nielsen. It ran eight hours with 42 performers.

Going in person?

It's free, but there are strict rules: no bags, no bathrooms, and once you leave you can't get back in. Read our Times Square New Year's Eve in person guide.

Quick answers

When does New Year's Rockin' Eve 2027 start?

Dick Clark's New Year's Rockin' Eve with Ryan Seacrest 2027 airs live on Thursday, December 31, 2026, beginning at 8 p.m. ET (listed as 8/7c) on ABC and Disney+, according to Dick Clark Productions.

Is New Year's Rockin' Eve on Disney+?

Yes. For the first time in the show's history, the 2027 edition will simulcast on Disney+ alongside the live ABC broadcast.

Who is hosting New Year's Eve 2027 on ABC?

Ryan Seacrest hosts. Rita Ora co hosts from Times Square, comedian Druski joins as the Chicago co host, and Julianne Hough and Rob Gronkowski co host from Las Vegas.

Who is performing on New Year's Rockin' Eve 2027?

The performer lineup has not been announced yet. Dick Clark Productions says more details will come in the coming months. Our lineup tracker adds names as they are officially announced.

What time does the ball drop in Central time?

The ball drops at midnight Eastern, which is 11 p.m. Central on Thursday, December 31. It starts its 60 second descent at 11:59 p.m. Eastern.

How many people watched Rockin' Eve last year?

Dick Clark Productions, citing Nielsen, says the 2026 edition reached 46 million total viewers, and more than 30 million watched the Times Square Ball Drop at midnight.
