What's trending in Pokémon Delta Reign and 30th Celebration

Nov 6🎮 Gaming

Pokémon's 30th Celebration and Delta Reign

Nov 6 is a huge day for collectors: the Delta Reign set (Elite Trainer Box $49.99) and the 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collections (Day with Espeon ex, Night with Umbreon ex, reported $179.99 each) both launch. Prerelease events start Oct 24 at local game stores, and Pokémon Center pre-orders sold out in about two hours.

The quick version

Mark Friday, November 6, 2026. That's when Delta Reign, the sixth English Mega Evolution set, and the 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collections (Day and Night) both hit shelves. Prerelease events start October 24 at local game stores. Pokémon Center pre-orders sold out in about two hours, so here's what each product costs at MSRP and how to avoid overpaying.

Why it's such a big deal

It's the Pokémon TCG's 30th anniversary, and the Ultra-Premium Collections lean hard into nostalgia, with a special pack of three Classic Collection cards and Pikachu ex promos illustrated by YOSHIROTTEN. Pair that with the last big set of 2026 and the start of holiday shopping, and you get queues, sellouts and a lot of marked-up listings.

How to buy at retail

  • Ask your local game store about prerelease seats (Oct 24) and pre-orders.
  • Watch the Pokémon Center and big-box stores like Target, Walmart, Best Buy and GameStop. Printed Price reported GameStop's Delta Reign ETB pre-order at $89.99 and Walmart and Amazon listings from third-party sellers only, all above MSRP, as of Oct 9.
  • Presale and resale prices usually cool once product ships. If you're buying a gift, MSRP is the number to beat.
  • Buy sealed product from the retailer itself, and be wary of resealed or fake boxes from unknown sellers.

Quick answers

When does Delta Reign come out?

Friday, November 6, 2026. Prerelease events start October 24 at participating game stores.

When is the Pokémon 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collection released?

November 6, 2026, at the Pokémon Center and where Pokémon TCG products are sold.

How much is the Delta Reign Elite Trainer Box?

$49.99 MSRP, or $59.99 for the Pokémon Center version with extra packs and a second promo.

What's in the 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collection?

Two foil promos (Pikachu ex plus Espeon ex or Umbreon ex), 29 30th Celebration booster packs, one Classic Collection pack, a playmat, 65 sleeves, a deck box, a metal coin, six dice and a TCG Live code.

Should I pay resale prices for Delta Reign?

Prices above MSRP often soften after release day. Compare with retail before you buy.
