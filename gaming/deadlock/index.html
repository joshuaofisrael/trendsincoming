Deadlock is Valve's hero shooter and MOBA hybrid, and it keeps setting records even though access is still limited. It passed Team Fortress 2's all-time Steam peak on October 9 and hit about 303,000 concurrent players on October 16, per SteamDB.

Why the numbers keep climbing

New heroes and big patches bring lapsed players back, and every record becomes its own headline, which pulls in curious newcomers. PC Gamer tied the TF2 milestone to a new hero drop, and a fresh hero vote is keeping the community busy.
