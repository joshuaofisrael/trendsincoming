The quick version

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 launches Friday, October 23, 2026 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC (Steam, Battle.net and Xbox on PC) and Nintendo Switch 2. Digital pre-order players have had the full 16-mission campaign since October 16, and DMZ early access starts today, October 20.

How early access works

Buy or pre-order the digital Standard or Vault Edition on any supported platform and the whole campaign unlocks right away. DMZ opens to the same players from October 20, and everyone else jumps in on October 23. Campaign completion rewards include operators like Park, Price's crew and Double XP tokens that carry over to Multiplayer and DMZ at launch.

Launch-week tips

  • Preload now and clear storage space before Friday.
  • Add friends by Activision ID so your squad is ready for crossplay.
  • Playing the campaign first? It's a great way to bank Double XP tokens before Multiplayer opens.
