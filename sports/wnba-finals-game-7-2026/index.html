What's trending in WNBA Finals Game 7 (Oct 31, 2026)

The quick version

The WNBA Finals are going the distance. The Atlanta Dream beat the Golden State Valkyries 80-69 in Game 6 on Thursday at State Farm Arena to tie the series 3-3, per WNBA.com. Game 7 is Saturday, October 31, at 8 p.m. ET (5 p.m. PT; midnight GMT) at Chase Center in San Francisco, on USA and Peacock. ESPN says it's the first Game 7 in WNBA Finals history.
