The quick version

The Golden State Valkyries are WNBA champions. They beat the Atlanta Dream 68-63 on Saturday, October 31, at Chase Center in the first Game 7 in WNBA Finals history, winning the series 4-3, per ESPN. Gabby Williams scored 30 points, 28 of them in the second half, and was named Finals MVP.

FranchiseGolden State's first title, in its second season

How Golden State won it

Williams took over after halftime. She outscored the Dream 15-14 by herself in the third quarter and ran off 15 straight points across the third and fourth, which ESPN, citing Elias, says no player had done in a WNBA Finals game before. Her 30 points tied the record for a winner-take-all Finals game, and her 28 in the second half were the most in any half of a WNBA postseason game, per ESPN.

Why it is a landmark

Per ESPN and Elias, the Valkyries are the third team across the NBA, BAA and WNBA to win a title within their first two seasons, and the first WNBA champion without a single All-WNBA selection that season. Williams also had three 25-point games in the series, tied for the most in a single WNBA Finals.
