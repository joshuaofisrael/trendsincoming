Brewers sweep the Dodgers

Milwaukee is going to the World Series for the first time since 1982. They swept the two-time champs, capped by a 6-4 comeback in Game 4 on Garrett Mitchell's go-ahead homer. Jake Bauers was NLCS MVP.

The quick version

Final: Brewers 4, Dodgers 0. Milwaukee swept the two-time defending champs and won the pennant on October 15 at Dodger Stadium. The Brewers trailed at some point in all four games and came back every time. Jake Bauers was named NLCS MVP.

The swings that decided it

  • Game 1: Brice Turang's solo homer tied it, Luis Lara's single won it, and the bullpen got the last 14 outs.
  • Game 2: William Contreras homered to tie it in the fifth and singled in the go-ahead run in the sixth.
  • Game 3: Down 2-1 in the seventh, Bauers hit a two-run homer and Garrett Mitchell followed with a solo shot.
  • Game 4: Down 4-0 after Shohei Ohtani's three-run homer, Milwaukee clawed back, Mitchell homered for the lead in the sixth and Aaron Ashby threw the last three scoreless innings.
