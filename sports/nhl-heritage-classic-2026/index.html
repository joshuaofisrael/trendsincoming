The quick version

Final: Canadiens 2, Jets 1. Jacob Fowler made 33 saves as Montreal held on at the 2026 Tim Hortons NHL Heritage Classic at Princess Auto Stadium on Sunday, NHL.com reports. Lane Hutson and Kaiden Guhle scored in the second period, and Isak Rosen got Winnipeg's goal late in the third. At 21 years, 335 days, Fowler is the youngest goalie ever to start an outdoor NHL game. The 2026 Tim Hortons NHL Heritage Classic was Sunday, October 25: the Montreal Canadiens visit the Winnipeg Jets outdoors at Princess Auto Stadium. Puck drop is 7 p.m. ET (6 p.m. CT). In the U.S., watch on TNT, truTV and HBO Max; in Canada, on Sportsnet and TVAS. It's the first Jets game since Winnipeg traded star goalie Connor Hellebuyck to New Jersey on Friday, NHL.com reports.

The Hellebuyck backstory

Winnipeg traded Connor Hellebuyck to the New Jersey Devils on Friday for forwards Dawson Mercer and Nick Bjugstad, goalie Nico Daws, prospect Alexander Command and two draft picks, NHL.com reports. Hellebuyck had been suspended after refusing to report to camp following a trade request. "I think relief, just having clarity on the situation," Jets captain Adam Lowry said. Mercer is expected to play tonight on a line with Lowry.
