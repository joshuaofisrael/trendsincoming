The quick version

The Milwaukee Brewers are World Series champions for the first time. They beat the Tampa Bay Rays 4-1 in Game 5 on Wednesday, October 28, at Tropicana Field to take the series 4-1, MLB.com reports. Catcher William Contreras was named World Series MVP. The championship parade is today, Friday, October 30, at 10 a.m. CT (15:00 GMT) in downtown Milwaukee, with a free rally afterward.

How they won it

Misiorowski and the bullpen held Tampa Bay to four hits in the clincher, per MLB.com. Contreras' sixth-inning double scored Christian Yelich with an insurance run, and Brewers pitchers posted a 1.91 ERA for the series, MLB.com notes. "I don't have words to say how I'm feeling," Contreras said through an interpreter.

Parade today: time, route and how to watch

The parade starts at 10 a.m. CT at N. 4th St. and W. Wisconsin Ave. and ends with a free rally at Henry Maier Festival Park, per MLB.com. It streams on MLB Network, MLB.TV, Brewers.TV and free on Brewers.com. CBS Sports says rally gates open at 8 a.m. and the first 50,000 fans get a towel.

The Rays' side

"I'm crushed right now, disappointed," Rays manager Kevin Cash said, per MLB.com. Tampa Bay won Game 4 in 12 innings to extend the series a night.
