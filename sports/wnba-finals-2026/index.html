The quick version

The Golden State Valkyries lead the 2026 WNBA Finals 3-2 after beating the Atlanta Dream 64-55 in Game 5 on Monday at Chase Center. Kiah Stokes had 13 points, 8 rebounds and 3 blocks, and Golden State has a WNBA-record 37 blocks in a Finals, the team says. Game 6 is Thursday, October 29, at 12:30 p.m. ET (9:30 a.m. PT) at State Farm Arena in Atlanta on USA and Peacock.
