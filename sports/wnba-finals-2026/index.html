The quick version

The Atlanta Dream lead the 2026 WNBA Finals 2-1. They routed the Golden State Valkyries 97-71 in Game 3 on October 21 at State Farm Arena, leading by as many as 26. Angel Reese had 21 points and 12 rebounds. Game 4 is today, Saturday, October 24, at 1 p.m. ET in Atlanta on NBC and Peacock. The series is best of seven, so Atlanta needs two more wins for its first title.

How Game 3 got away from Golden State

Atlanta controlled it from early on and stretched the lead to 26, per AP. Reese posted another double-double, and Gabby Williams led the Valkyries with 15. Game 4 is Saturday afternoon in Atlanta, and Game 5 shifts back to San Francisco on Monday. Atlanta needs two more wins; Golden State needs three of the next four.
