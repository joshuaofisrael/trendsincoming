The quick version

The 2026 WNBA Finals are tied 2-2. The Golden State Valkyries beat the Atlanta Dream 67-62 in Game 4 on Saturday in Atlanta, led by Gabby Williams' 17 points and Kiah Stokes' 16 rebounds and three blocks, AP reports. Atlanta shot 33.3% and Angel Reese was held to 8 points. Game 5 is tonight, Monday, October 26, at 8 p.m. ET (5 p.m. PT) at Chase Center in San Francisco on USA and Peacock. Kiah Stokes set a Finals record with 14 blocks in the series, the Valkyries say.

The Valkyries led by 16 at halftime and held on as Atlanta went 4 of 23 from three. Tiffany Hayes scored all 13 of her points in the second half, and Rhyne Howard led the Dream with 19, per AP. Golden State now has home court for Game 5.
