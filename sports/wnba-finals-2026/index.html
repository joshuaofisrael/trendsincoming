The quick version

The 2026 WNBA Finals are tied 1-1. Angel Reese had 30 points and 16 rebounds and Rhyne Howard set a Finals record with six steals as the Atlanta Dream beat the Golden State Valkyries 79-65 in Game 2 on October 19, the franchise's first-ever Finals win. Game 3 is Wednesday, October 21, at 8 p.m. ET at State Farm Arena in Atlanta on USA and Peacock. Neither team has won a title, so a first-time champion is guaranteed.

Why Game 2 flipped the series

Atlanta forced 19 turnovers and scored 24 points off them, then broke a 40-40 tie in the third quarter with a 29-8 run. Golden State shot 34.4%, and its bench outscored Atlanta's 33-11, but only Gabby Williams reached double figures among the starters. It was the Valkyries' first regulation loss since August 21.
