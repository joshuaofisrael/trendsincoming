NBA Cup 2026: schedule, standings and how to watch every round

By The Trends Incoming team · Oct 16, 2026

The Emirates NBA Cup is back for year four, and the final has a brand new home: Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis, the legendary gym from Hoosiers. Group Play starts Friday, October 30, and the trophy gets handed out on Friday, December 11.

🗓️ Key dates

Group Play (Cup Nights)Fridays Oct 30, Nov 6, 13, 20 and 27, plus Tue Nov 24 and Wed Nov 25
QuarterfinalsFri Dec 4 and Sat Dec 5 (home court for the higher seed)
SemifinalsTue Dec 8 and/or Wed Dec 9. Exact date not set yet.
ChampionshipFri Dec 11, Hinkle Fieldhouse, Indianapolis. Tickets and travel →

📺 How to watch

  • Group Play: each Friday's headline games are on Prime Video, the rest on NBA League Pass. Tue Nov 24 is on NBC and Peacock. Wed Nov 25 has three ESPN games.
  • Quarterfinals, semifinals and the Championship: Prime Video only. No cable, no League Pass.

How it works in 30 seconds

Six groups of five. Four group games each. Win your group, or be the best runner-up in your conference, and you're in the final eight. Ties are broken by head-to-head, then point differential. From there it's single elimination all the way to Indy.

Quick answers

When does the NBA Cup 2026 start?

Group Play tips off Friday, October 30, 2026.

How does the NBA Cup work?

All 30 teams are split into six groups of five. Each team plays the other four in its group on Cup Nights. The three group winners in each conference plus one wild card (the best second-place team) reach the eight-team quarterfinals. Group games also count toward the regular season.

Is the NBA Cup on Prime Video?

Yes. Prime Video carries several Group Play games on Cup Nights and is the only place to watch the Knockout Rounds, including the Championship. Other Group Play games are on NBA League Pass, NBC, Peacock and ESPN.

When and where is the NBA Cup final in 2026?

Friday, December 11, 2026, at Hinkle Fieldhouse on Butler University's campus in Indianapolis. It's the first Cup final held outside Las Vegas.

When are the NBA Cup quarterfinals and semifinals?

Quarterfinals are December 4 and 5. Semifinals are December 8 and/or 9, with the exact date still to be set.
