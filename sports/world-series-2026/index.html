The quick version

The Milwaukee Brewers lead the World Series 1-0 after holding off the Tampa Bay Rays 3-2 in Game 1 on Friday night. Christian Yelich tripled in the first run, and Trevor Megill struck out Jonny DeLuca with two runners in scoring position to end it, after a replay review overturned what was first called a game-ending double play, AP reports. Game 2 is tonight, Saturday, October 24, at 8 p.m. ET in Milwaukee on FOX: Logan Henderson for the Brewers against Drew Rasmussen for the Rays.

How Game 1 played out

Milwaukee jumped out early: Yelich's triple scored Jackson Chourio and William Contreras singled home Yelich in the first. Tampa Bay got one back in the fourth, and Chourio's RBI single made it 3-1 in the seventh. In the ninth the Rays loaded the bases with a double steal and a walk, and Liam Hicks' grounder was ruled a game-ending double play until replay showed Cooper Pratt's foot was slightly off the bag. Megill then struck out DeLuca. The Rays went 0 for 6 with runners in scoring position, while Jonathan Aranda reached base four times, per AP.

Quick answers

Who won Game 1 of the 2026 World Series?

The Milwaukee Brewers beat the Tampa Bay Rays 3-2 on October 23, 2026, at American Family Field.

When is World Series Game 2?

Saturday, October 24, 2026, at 8 p.m. ET in Milwaukee, on FOX, FOX Deportes and FOX One.

Who is pitching Game 2 of the World Series?

Logan Henderson for Milwaukee and Drew Rasmussen for Tampa Bay, per MLB.com and NBC Sports.

What happened at the end of World Series Game 1?

A game-ending double play was overturned on replay because shortstop Cooper Pratt's foot was off the bag, a run scored, and then Trevor Megill struck out Jonny DeLuca to finish the 3-2 win, AP reports.

Have the Brewers or Rays ever won the World Series?

No. Milwaukee lost its only previous World Series in 1982, and Tampa Bay lost in 2008 and 2020.
