The quick version

The Milwaukee Brewers lead the World Series 2-0 after rallying from a 4-0 first-inning hole to beat the Tampa Bay Rays 5-4 in Game 2 on Saturday. Sal Frelick tied it with a sixth-inning home run and Cooper Pratt singled home the go-ahead run in the seventh, AP reports. Game 3 is tonight, Monday, October 26, at 8 p.m. ET at Tropicana Field on FOX and FOX One: Freddy Peralta for Tampa Bay against Robert Gasser for Milwaukee.

How Game 2 played out

Tampa Bay scored four in the first on RBI singles from Simpson and Caminero and a two-run homer by Liam Hicks. Milwaukee answered with two in the bottom of the first and one in the fourth. Sal Frelick tied it 4-4 with a sixth-inning homer off Ian Seymour, his first since July 11, and Cooper Pratt's single off Kevin Kelly in the seventh scored Brice Turang with the winning run. Aaron Ashby struck out Yandy Diaz to end it. "They believe and they don't give up," manager Pat Murphy said, per AP. Milwaukee has won seven straight.
