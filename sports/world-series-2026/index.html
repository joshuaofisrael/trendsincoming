The quick version

The Milwaukee Brewers won the 2026 World Series, beating the Tampa Bay Rays 4-1 in Game 5 on Wednesday, October 28, at Tropicana Field to take the series 4-1. It's the first title in franchise history, MLB.com reports, and catcher William Contreras is World Series MVP. The parade is today at 10 a.m. CT.
