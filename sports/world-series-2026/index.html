Game 1 storylines

Misiorowski threw 1,115 pitches of 100 mph or more this season, per MLB.com. Milwaukee hosts Games 1 and 2, then the series moves to St. Petersburg for Game 3 on Monday, October 26. Christian Yelich told MLB.com that "our performance directly affects people's happiness and togetherness." Our Game 1 cheat sheet has everything in one place.

Why it's a big deal

Both fan bases have waited a long time. The Brewers lost the 1982 World Series in seven games, and the Rays lost in 2008 and 2020. Somebody's drought ends this fall. Milwaukee has home-field advantage, hosting Games 1, 2, 6 and 7.
