The quick version

It's Rays vs. Brewers, and baseball is guaranteed a first-time champion: neither franchise has ever won a World Series. Tampa Bay clinched the AL pennant with a 7-3 win over Cleveland in Game 6 on October 19, and Milwaukee swept the Dodgers. Game 1 is Friday, October 23, at 8 p.m. ET at American Family Field in Milwaukee on FOX, with Drew Rasmussen expected to face Jacob Misiorowski.

Why it's a big deal

Both fan bases have waited a long time. The Brewers lost the 1982 World Series in seven games, and the Rays lost in 2008 and 2020. Somebody's drought ends this fall. Milwaukee has home-field advantage, hosting Games 1, 2, 6 and 7.
