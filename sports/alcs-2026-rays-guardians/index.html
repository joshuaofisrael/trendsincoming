The quick version

The Tampa Bay Rays are going to the World Series. They beat Cleveland 7-3 in Game 6 at Tropicana Field on Monday, October 19, to win the ALCS 4-2. Freddy Peralta allowed one run over 5 1/3 innings, Junior Caminero hit a two-run homer in the seventh, and Díaz added a two-run shot in the eighth. Next up: the Milwaukee Brewers, starting Friday, October 23.

Game 6 in a nutshell

The Rays grabbed a 2-0 lead in the second on a two-run double, added a sacrifice fly in the third, and pulled away late. Cleveland scored twice in the eighth to make it 5-3 before Díaz's 416-foot homer put it away. Tampa Bay won three of the last four games.
