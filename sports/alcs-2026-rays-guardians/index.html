How we got here

Tampa Bay was the top team in the American League at 98-64, and Cleveland (85-77) came in as the underdog after a comeback against the White Sox in the Division Series. The Guardians stole Game 1 on the road, the Rays answered behind Freddy Peralta in Game 2, and rookie Petey Halpin's two-run homer won Game 3 for Cleveland. Then the rain came. Friday's Game 4 was postponed, and on Saturday the Rays turned two Cleveland errors into runs, with Jonathan Aranda's seventh-inning single and a Yandy Díaz two-run homer in the ninth sealing it.

Game 5 in a nutshell

The Rays scored once in each of the first three innings, and Rasmussen did the rest, allowing one run over seven innings. Cleveland had five hits all night. Now Tampa Bay goes home needing one win in two tries, with Cleveland ace Gavin Williams reported unavailable for Game 6.

Our take

This has been a pitching-and-defense series, with no team scoring more than seven runs and two of four games decided by a run or two. Whoever avoids the mistakes tonight probably wins. Either way, the Brewers get a well-rested look at the winner.
