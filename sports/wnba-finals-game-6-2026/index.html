The quick version

The Golden State Valkyries can win the WNBA title on Thursday. They lead the Atlanta Dream 3-2, and Game 6 tips at 12:30 p.m. ET (9:30 a.m. PT, 16:30 GMT) on Thursday, October 29, at State Farm Arena in Atlanta, on USA and Peacock. The game was first set for 8 p.m. ET and moved so it could be played in the bigger arena, NBC Sports reports.

Why it's at lunchtime

The Dream's home games in these Finals have moved from the Gateway Center Arena to State Farm Arena, and the Atlanta Hawks play there Thursday night, so Game 6 starts at 12:30 p.m. ET and the Hawks moved their tip to 8 p.m., NBC Sports explains.

Where the series stands

Golden State won Game 5 64-55 at Chase Center behind 13 points each from Kiah Stokes and Veronica Burton, per the Valkyries' recap. Rhyne Howard scored 20 for Atlanta. A Valkyries win gives the franchise its first title; a Dream win forces Game 7 on Saturday.
