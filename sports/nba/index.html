When does the 2026-27 NBA season start?

Tuesday, October 20, 2026, with three games on NBC and Peacock.

Where can I watch national NBA games?

On NBC and Peacock, ESPN and ABC, and Prime Video, depending on the night. Every national game streams on the ESPN app, Peacock or Prime Video.
