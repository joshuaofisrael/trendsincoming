The quick version

The 2026-27 NBA season is underway. On opening night, Tuesday, October 20, the Philadelphia 76ers beat the champion Knicks 106-100 at Madison Square Garden right after New York raised its title banner. Tyrese Maxey scored 28, Jaylen Brown had 19 in his Sixers debut, and LeBron James opened his record 24th season with 14 points, 9 rebounds and 7 assists. Earlier, Detroit came back from 22 points down to beat Boston 121-113, and Oklahoma City beat San Antonio 116-104 in the nightcap.

The Knicks got the rings and the Sixers got the win.
