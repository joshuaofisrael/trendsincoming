The quick version

The Supreme Court's October 2026 term is underway. Two cases argued this month are getting attention: Beaird v. United States (October 13), about how much weight courts give the Sentencing Commission's commentary, and Salazar v. Paramount Global (October 14), about how far the Video Privacy Protection Act reaches. Decisions in argued cases are due by the end of the term, usually late June or early July.

Why these cases matter

Beaird could change how sentencing guidelines are applied in federal courts, and justices from both wings sounded skeptical of a big change at argument, per Courthouse News. Salazar matters for any website or app that embeds video and shares viewing data, which is a lot of the internet.
