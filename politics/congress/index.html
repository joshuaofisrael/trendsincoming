The quick version

No shutdown on Halloween. Federal funding runs through December 11, 2026 under H.R. 6500, signed September 2. That puts the next deadline in the lame-duck session right after the November 3 midterms. Here's what's on the must-pass list.

Funding lawContinuing Appropriations and Extensions Act, 2027 (H.R. 6500)
SignedSep 2, 2026
Funding runs throughFri Dec 11, 2026
MidtermsTue Nov 3, 2026
Session after the electionLame duck, November to early January

What could happen by December 11

  • A big full-year funding package (an omnibus).
  • Another short-term extension into 2027.
  • A mix: some full-year bills, a stopgap for the rest.

If talks stall, agencies without approved funding pause non-essential work and some federal workers are furloughed, while essential services continue.

Quick answers

Is there a government shutdown on October 31, 2026?

No. H.R. 6500, signed September 2, 2026, funds federal agencies through December 11, 2026.

When is the next government funding deadline?

December 11, 2026, after the November 3 midterm elections.

What happens in a government shutdown?

Agencies without approved funding pause non-essential work and some federal workers are furloughed. Essential services continue.
