Midterms: 16 days out

Every House seat, 35 Senate races and 36 governor races are on the ballot Nov 3. This week's polls cut both ways: Bowling Green/YouGov has Brown (D) up 48-42 in Ohio, InsiderAdvantage has Collins (R) trailing Jackson (D) 49-47 in Maine, and the same pollster has Paxton (R) up 49-48 in Texas. Most margins are inside the error.

Dec 11🏛️ Politics

Shutdown watch: the real deadline is Dec 11

Correction: there is no Oct 31 funding cliff. The stopgap law signed Sept 2 (H.R. 6500) funds the government through Dec 11, 2026. Congress is out until after the election, and leaders in both parties will face that deadline in the lame-duck session.
