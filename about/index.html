About Trends Incoming

Trends Incoming is your curator for what's trending right now. The internet throws a thousand things at you every day. We pick the ones that actually matter: the drop, the trailer, the chart-topper, the deadline, the meme your cousin's going to send you tomorrow.

Every card gets a short take on why it's trending, plus links to the original source and the conversation on TikTok, Instagram, X and more.

Editorial policy

  • Sources first. Dates, prices, figures and quotes come from official sources (agencies, leagues, studios, companies) or established news outlets, and we link them on the page. When something isn't confirmed, we say so.
  • Official vs projected. Numbers that aren't final, like tax projections, are labeled as projections, with whose estimate it is and how it was made.
  • Neutral on politics. We cover elections and policy without endorsing candidates or parties, and link sources from across the spectrum.
  • No advice. Money, health and legal pages are general information, not personal advice. Stock pages carry a full disclaimer.
  • Independent. We're not affiliated with the brands, platforms or leagues we cover. If we ever use affiliate links or ads, we'll disclose them on the page and update our privacy policy first.
  • Dates you can trust. Each page shows when it was last updated, and its first-published date when that's different.

Corrections policy

If we get something wrong, we fix it fast. Factual errors are corrected on the page as soon as we confirm them, and the page's updated date changes. Significant corrections (a wrong date, number or name that changes the meaning) get a short note on the page saying what changed. We don't quietly rewrite history.
