🔥 Spoiler-free refresher

Panem is a country split into a glittering Capitol and twelve struggling districts. Every year, each district must send two teenagers into a televised fight for survival called the Hunger Games. It's a punishment for an old rebellion, dressed up as entertainment.

Every 25th year is a "Quarter Quell" with an extra-cruel twist. Sunrise on the Reaping goes back to the 50th Games, the second Quell, 24 years before Katniss. Our lead is a teenage Haymitch Abernathy from District 12. If you only know him as Katniss's grumpy, sharp-tongued mentor, this is the story of how he got that way. It's based on Suzanne Collins's 2025 novel.
