The quick version

You Can See Everything, Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim's A24 documentary about Elizabeth Holmes, is heading for $12 million to $14 million in its opening weekend from just 855 theaters. That would be the second-best documentary opening ever, behind Fahrenheit 9/11's $23.9 million in 2004.

Why it's selling out

Deadline reported about 1,300 sellouts by Saturday morning and lines around the block in New York and Nashville. Reportedly three-quarters of New York and Los Angeles buyers were under 35, which is wild for a three-hour nonfiction film. Fielder fans, true-crime fans and people who watched The Dropout all showed up at once.

Can I stream it?

Not yet. It's theaters only. A24 has a showtimes finder on its site.
