The quick version

Clayface, the DC Studios horror movie about the classic Batman villain, opens in U.S. theaters Friday, October 23, 2026 from Warner Bros. Tom Rhys Harries plays Matt Hagen, an actor whose face is disfigured by a mob boss, who volunteers for an experiment that turns his body into living clay. The first reactions on October 14 called it "freakishly awesome" and a hard R.

Who is Clayface?

Clayface is one of Batman's oldest villains. The first version, actor Basil Karlo, appeared in 1940, and the shape-shifting Matt Hagen version debuted in Detective Comics in 1961. This movie goes with Hagen: a performer who loses his face, then loses himself, which is why critics keep comparing it to body-horror hits like The Substance.

What early viewers said

Collider's Steven Weintraub said it "doesn't require any knowledge of DC to enjoy" and is "loaded with brutal scenes." Others praised Harries's performance and the mix of horror and gothic romance. A few called parts of it silly. Translation: if you like gory, ambitious horror, this is your Friday night. If you're squeamish, maybe not.

Quick answers

When does Clayface come out?

Friday, October 23, 2026, in U.S. theaters from Warner Bros.

Who plays Clayface?

Tom Rhys Harries plays Matt Hagen, the actor who becomes Clayface. Naomi Ackie plays scientist Dr. Caitlyn Corr.

Is Clayface rated R?

Early reactions describe it as a hard R with intense body horror. Check the official rating on your theater listing.

Do I need to see other DC movies before Clayface?

No. Early viewers say it works without any DC knowledge.

Who directed Clayface?

James Watkins, who made Speak No Evil. Mike Flanagan wrote the story and co-wrote the script with Hossein Amini.
