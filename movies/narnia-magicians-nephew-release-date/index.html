📅 The date, and why it moved

Narnia was originally a Thanksgiving 2026 release. On May 1, 2026, Netflix moved it to February 12, 2027 for a wide, global theatrical release, with IMAX-only sneak previews from February 10. It's Netflix's first movie to get a global theatrical release, and it lands on Netflix itself on April 2, 2027.

Fun timing: opening weekend is also Super Bowl weekend. Super Bowl LXI is Sunday, Feb 14, 2027, which is Valentine's Day too. Big weekend for group chats.

🎬 The trailer

The first trailer dropped Tuesday, October 13, 2026, after Gerwig showed it at a New York preview event hosted with Stephen Colbert. It's set to Led Zeppelin's Stairway to Heaven, which Netflix says the band had never licensed for a trailer before. Highlights: Daniel Craig as Uncle Andrew, Emma Mackey's Jadis, Fledge the flying horse, and Aslan, voiced by Meryl Streep, singing Narnia into being. Netflix's Tudum feature with new photos followed on October 15.

📚 Reading order: story vs publication

The big debate. Netflix lists the series in story order, starting with The Magician's Nephew. Lewis actually published it sixth, in 1955. Either order works. Story order follows Narnia from creation to the end; publication order lets you meet Narnia through the wardrobe first, like the original readers did.

Quick answers

When does the new Narnia movie come out?

Narnia: The Magician's Nephew opens in IMAX and theaters on Friday, February 12, 2027. IMAX-only sneak previews start Wednesday, February 10, 2027.

Is the new Narnia on Netflix?

Yes, later. It streams on Netflix starting April 2, 2027, after its theatrical run. Netflix handles distribution in North America and Sony Pictures distributes outside North America.

Why was Narnia delayed?

It was set for Thanksgiving 2026. On May 1, 2026 it moved to February 12, 2027 for a wider global theatrical release with a full theatrical window, according to Variety and IMAX's statement.

Is there a Narnia trailer?

Yes. The first trailer came out on Tuesday, October 13, 2026, set to Led Zeppelin's "Stairway to Heaven". Netflix's Tudum feature with the trailer, photos and cast followed on October 15.

Do I need to read The Magician's Nephew first?

No. It's the origin story of Narnia, so it works as a starting point. It was published sixth, in 1955, but comes first in story order, and Netflix lists it first in the series.
