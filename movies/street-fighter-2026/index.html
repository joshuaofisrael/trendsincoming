The quick version

Paramount and Legendary's live-action Street Fighter opened October 16, 2026 and is projected to earn about $31 million in its first weekend from 3,575 theaters, enough for No. 1. It's in theaters only for now.

Who's showing up

Deadline says the crowd skews older and male, with 45% of ticket buyers over 35, which is also why it hurt Whalefall's opening. Critics are lukewarm, audiences are much kinder, and that gap is classic for video game movies. If you grew up mashing buttons in arcades, you are the target.

Where to watch

In theaters now, including IMAX and premium screens in many markets. No streaming date has been announced.
