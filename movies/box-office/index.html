The quick version

Street Fighter won the weekend of October 16 to 18 with an estimated $31 million ($51.1 million worldwide), and A24's You Can See Everything took $15 million from just 855 theaters, the second-biggest non-concert documentary opening ever. These are Sunday studio estimates; actuals arrive Monday.

1. Street Fighter (Paramount)$31M, 3,575 theaters; $51.1M worldwide
2. You Can See Everything (A24)$15M, 855 theaters, about $17,500 per theater
3. Other Mommy (Universal)$8.35M in weekend two (down 70%), $44.85M total
4. Whalefall (20th Century)$8M opening
5. Sense and Sensibility (Focus)$7.25M opening
6. Verity (Amazon MGM)$6.21M, $66.1M total

The takeaways

Street Fighter landed close to forecasts: solid, not a breakout. The Numbers notes it sits between Masters of the Universe and Red One among recent openings. The real surprise is a three-hour documentary about Elizabeth Holmes doing per-theater business of about $15,000, on track for the second-best documentary opening ever behind Fahrenheit 9/11. Whalefall is the weekend's bellyflop against a reported $60 million budget.
