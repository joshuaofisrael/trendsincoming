The quick version

Street Fighter is No. 1 this weekend (October 16 to 18) with a projected $31 million, and A24's You Can See Everything is the story of the frame at $12 million to $14 million from just 855 theaters. These are Saturday projections; studios report Sunday estimates around midday Eastern, and final numbers land Monday.

1. Street Fighter (Paramount)$31M projected, 3,575 theaters
2. You Can See Everything (A24)$12M to $14M projected, 855 theaters
3. Other Mommy (Universal)About $7.85M in weekend two, $44.3M total
4. Whalefall (20th Century)About $7.5M opening

The takeaways

Street Fighter landed close to forecasts: solid, not a breakout. The Numbers notes it sits between Masters of the Universe and Red One among recent openings. The real surprise is a three-hour documentary about Elizabeth Holmes doing per-theater business of about $15,000, on track for the second-best documentary opening ever behind Fahrenheit 9/11. Whalefall is the weekend's bellyflop against a reported $60 million budget.
