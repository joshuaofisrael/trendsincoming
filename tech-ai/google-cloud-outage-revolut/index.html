The quick version

If your Revolut app died on Tuesday, you weren't alone. On October 20, 2026, a power event in Google Cloud's London region (europe-west2) caused network failures that took down Revolut's app, card payments, ATM withdrawals, transfers and sign-in around the world, and knocked other UK services offline too. Google says it has resolved the outage, and Revolut says services are restored and customers' money and data stayed safe.

What to do the next time your bank app goes down

  1. Check the company's official status page or social accounts before you panic. Outages usually get acknowledged within minutes.
  2. Carry a backup: a second card from a different bank, or a little cash, covers you at the checkout.
  3. Don't retry a payment over and over. Wait for service to return, then check your transactions for duplicates.
  4. Ignore texts or calls offering to "fix" your account during an outage. Scammers love these moments, and your bank will never ask for your passcode.

Why one cloud problem breaks so much

Many apps rent computing power from a few giant cloud providers. When one region has trouble, every company that leans on it can wobble at once. Big firms spread work across several regions to avoid exactly this, so expect questions about backup plans after Tuesday.
