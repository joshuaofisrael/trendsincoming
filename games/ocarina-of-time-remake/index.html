Ocarina of Time remake lands Nov 5

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is reborn exclusively on Nintendo Switch 2 on November 5, 2026, one week after the Zelda 40th Anniversary Switch 2 hardware.

ReleaseNovember 5, 2026
PlatformNintendo Switch 2 only
Price$59.99 digital / $69.99 physical
