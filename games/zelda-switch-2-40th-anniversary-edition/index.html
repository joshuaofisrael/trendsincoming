Zelda 40th Anniversary Switch 2 drops Oct 29

Nintendo's first special-edition Switch 2, the Nintendo Switch 2 – The Legend of Zelda – 40th Anniversary Edition, releases October 29, 2026. Nintendo announced it in the Legend of Zelda 40th Anniversary Direct on September 8, 2026.

ReleaseOctober 29, 2026
US price$519.99 (Nintendo US store listing, as reported)
Matching Pro Controller$99.99; $109.99 bundle with display stand
Game included?No. Ocarina of Time (Nov 5) is sold separately.
Also launching Oct 29Zelda Pro Controller and carrying case & screen protector

Stock: US pre-orders sold out fast after the September 9 launch, and several retailers showed it out of stock in early October. Check Nintendo's store and major retailers on launch day.
