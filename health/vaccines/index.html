The quick version

It's prime time for fall shots. The CDC says everyone 6 months and older should get a flu shot, ideally by the end of October, and people 65 and older should get one of the higher-dose types. For COVID, CDC's current guidance is a shared decision with your health care provider, which matters most for people 65+ and those at higher risk.

Flu shotEveryone 6 months and older
Best timingBy the end of October, per CDC
65 and olderHigh-dose or other enhanced flu vaccines preferred
COVID shotShared decision with your provider
Where to get onePharmacies, your doctor, local health departments; vaccines.gov lists locations

Good to know

It takes about two weeks after a flu shot to build protection, which is why the end of October is the target. Most insurance covers flu shots with no copay. If you're sick with a fever, wait until you feel better.

General information, not medical advice. Ask your doctor or pharmacist what's right for you.
