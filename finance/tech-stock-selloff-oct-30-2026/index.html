The quick version

Tech stocks had a rough Friday. The S&P 500 fell about 1.8% on October 30 for its worst day since June, the Nasdaq dropped 2.9% and the Dow lost 1.1%, the Associated Press reported (via CBC News). The worry: whether hundreds of billions of dollars in AI spending will pay off. Not financial advice.

What happened

Intel led the way down after giving a fourth-quarter profit forecast below analysts' expectations. CEO Lip-Bu Tan pointed to pressure on PCs, per AP via CBC. A day earlier, Alphabet fell more than 12%, its worst day on record. "We're moving out of the hopes and dreams phase of the AI trade and into the 'show me' phase," Joe Tigay of Equity Armor Investments said in a note quoted by AP.

Not everything fell

Outside tech, some big names rose, AP reported: ExxonMobil gained 2.2%, Chevron 1.8%, Mondelez 3.9% and Colgate-Palmolive 2.3%. Crypto stocks tumbled after a hack at Binance, the world's biggest crypto exchange, and bitcoin fell toward $77,000.
