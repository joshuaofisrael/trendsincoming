Why it's trending

It lands in the busiest hour of earnings season. Stocks rose Tuesday, with the S&P 500 up 0.6% to 7,722.60 and the Nasdaq up 1.18%, and Alphabet and Microsoft report after Wednesday's close, CNBC reports. Oil stays high too: CNBC put WTI at $99.90 and Brent at $113.88 on Tuesday.

What a rate decision touches

The Fed sets a target for the overnight rate banks charge each other. Card APRs and many savings yields tend to move with it over time, while mortgage rates follow longer-term bond yields and can move before or after the Fed. That's general background, not a prediction for your own loans or savings.
