Japan's 9.9 quake alert that wasn't

Japan's weather agency sent an emergency alert for a magnitude 9.9 quake on Oct 16, then withdrew it about eight minutes later. The real quake was a 6.9 near Kamchatka. JMA apologized, explained the error and denied any cyberattack.
