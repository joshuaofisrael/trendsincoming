The quick version

Israel votes on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, for all 120 seats in the Knesset. A coalition needs 61. The final KAN and Channel 12 polls put Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's bloc at 50 seats and the opposition at 52, Anadolu reports, while the final Times of Israel (Zman Yisrael) poll showed the two camps tied at 51-51. Neither side reaches a majority in those polls, so coalition talks after the vote are likely to decide the next government.

What the final polls say

The last surveys allowed before the legal blackout put the race very close. Anadolu reports the KAN and Channel 12 polls with Yashar on 22 seats, Likud on 20 and Naftali Bennett's Together on 14, and the opposition bloc two seats ahead of Netanyahu's camp. The Times of Israel's final poll had the blocs dead even. Polls carry margins of error and small parties near the 3.25% threshold can swing the math.

What happens after the vote

The president asks the candidate with the best chance of forming a majority to try to build a coalition. If no one can, the Times of Israel notes, Israel could head to another election in early 2027, with Netanyahu remaining as caretaker prime minister in the meantime.
