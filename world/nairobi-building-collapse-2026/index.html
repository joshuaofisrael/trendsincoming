The quick version

A multistorey building under construction collapsed in Nairobi's busy Eastleigh district at about 1 p.m. local time on Friday, October 23, 2026. Kenya's interior minister said 67 workers were on site, with one injured and 66 unharmed, and rescuers reported contact with some survivors still trapped, Al Jazeera, AP and AFP report. People in shops on the lower floors were still unaccounted for.

What officials said

Nairobi Governor Johnson Sakaja said the shops on the ground floor should not have been open because the building had no occupation certificate. "Our concern is to save lives," he told reporters, per Al Jazeera. Police warned of a possible second collapse because the structure was leaning on its neighbor.

Why it matters

Kenya's National Building Inspectorate found that 87 percent of buildings audited nationwide last year failed to meet standards or lacked approvals, AFP reported. Officials said anyone found negligent would face the law. Numbers often change in the first days after a collapse, so rely on official updates and wire services.

Quick answers

What happened in Eastleigh, Nairobi?

A multistorey building under construction collapsed on Friday, October 23, 2026, at about 1 p.m. local time.

How many people are missing in the Nairobi building collapse?

About 11 people in ground-floor shops were unaccounted for on Friday, per Al Jazeera. Kenya's Daily Nation later reported seven missing. Figures may change.

Was anyone rescued from the Nairobi collapse?

Yes. The Kenya Red Cross said two injured people were rescued and taken to Kenyatta National Hospital, per local reports cited by Al Jazeera.

Why were people inside a building under construction?

Officials say the ground floor and basement had been rented to shops even though the building had no occupation certificate, which the governor said made it illegal.
