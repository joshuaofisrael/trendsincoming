The quick version

Brazilians are choosing their president today, Sunday, October 25, 2026, in a runoff between incumbent Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva and Sen. Flavio Bolsonaro. The two are neck and neck: a CNT/MDA poll on Saturday showed the senator with a 1.3-point lead, Reuters reports, after a stronger-than-expected first round for him. Brazil votes on electronic machines, so the count usually moves fast on election night. Official results are published by the electoral court, the TSE.

The final day

Lula, 80, who is seeking a fourth non-consecutive term, marched along Copacabana beach in Rio and along Paulista Avenue in Sao Paulo on Saturday. "We're going to win the election for one simple reason: it's true that the people want change, (but) they want to move forward," he told journalists, per Reuters. Flavio Bolsonaro joined a motorcycle caravan in Belo Horizonte and a march in Rio de Janeiro. "There are only a few hours left before we shape the future of our nation for the next 30 or 40 years," he said in a social media video, Reuters reports.

Context

Flavio Bolsonaro is the eldest son of former President Jair Bolsonaro, who was sentenced to more than 27 years in prison for plotting a coup after losing the 2022 election, Reuters notes. The two candidates met for a final TV Globo debate on Friday, sparring over the cost of living. Polls were close enough that the result may not be clear until late in the count.

Quick answers

When is the Brazil runoff?

Today, Sunday, October 25, 2026.

Who is in Brazil's presidential runoff?

President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva of the Workers' Party and Sen. Flavio Bolsonaro, the eldest son of former President Jair Bolsonaro.

Who is ahead in the Brazil runoff polls?

It is neck and neck. A CNT/MDA poll on October 24 showed Flavio Bolsonaro with a 1.3-point lead, per Reuters. Polls have margins of error.

Where can I see Brazil election results?

Brazil's electoral court, the TSE, publishes official live results at resultados.tse.jus.br.
