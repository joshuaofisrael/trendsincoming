The quick version

Results: Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva won Brazil's October 25 runoff, a fourth term as president. With all votes counted, Lula had 51.79% to 48.21% for Sen. Flavio Bolsonaro, per electoral court (TSE) figures reported by the BBC. Bolsonaro has not conceded: he called a two-hour extension of voting "inexplicable," although both campaigns had asked for it, and said he would seek to annul the result, the BBC reports. The new term begins January 5, 2027. Brazilians chose their president on Sunday, October 25, 2026, in a runoff between incumbent Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva and Sen. Flavio Bolsonaro. The two are neck and neck: a CNT/MDA poll on Saturday showed the senator with a 1.3-point lead, Reuters reports, after a stronger-than-expected first round for him. Brazil votes on electronic machines, so the count usually moves fast on election night. Official results are published by the electoral court, the TSE.

What happened

Lula came from behind after trailing in the first round on October 4, the BBC reports. Bolsonaro's Liberal Party made the biggest gains in Congress, which means the new government will need to negotiate in a divided legislature. Any challenge to the result goes through the electoral court, the TSE.

The final day

Lula, 80, who is seeking a fourth non-consecutive term, marched along Copacabana beach in Rio and along Paulista Avenue in Sao Paulo on Saturday. "We're going to win the election for one simple reason: it's true that the people want change, (but) they want to move forward," he told journalists, per Reuters. Flavio Bolsonaro joined a motorcycle caravan in Belo Horizonte and a march in Rio de Janeiro. "There are only a few hours left before we shape the future of our nation for the next 30 or 40 years," he said in a social media video, Reuters reports.

Context

Flavio Bolsonaro is the eldest son of former President Jair Bolsonaro. The two candidates met for a final TV Globo debate on Friday, sparring over the cost of living. Polls were close enough that the result may not be clear until late in the count.
