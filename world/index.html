U.S. allows Russian diesel sales through April 2027

Treasury issued a temporary license letting Russian diesel be sold through April 2027, AP reports. President Trump says it will help lower fuel prices and push Russia toward talks. Zelenskyy called it a weak decision that helps Moscow and urged Americans to back reinstating the sanctions, and the Kremlin welcomed the move.

France: 2,400 arrests after a weekend of riots

UK sends Typhoon jets to help defend Saudi Arabia

Britain is deploying RAF Typhoons in a defensive role after weeks of Houthi strikes on Saudi cities and oil sites, the BBC reports. The UK defense secretary said the jets will not carry out offensive strikes. Several airlines have paused flights to the region.

Hormuz: Iran floats a reopening plan

Oct 20🌍 World

Ebola: Kenya at nine cases, Nigeria confirms one

Kenya's health ministry now counts nine confirmed cases of Bundibugyo virus disease, a type of Ebola, including one death, all in a single cluster of people already in quarantine. Nigeria confirmed one imported case in Abuja on Oct 20, a traveler from Uganda now in isolation, with 44 contacts being monitored. The CDC has entry rules for U.S.-bound travelers from affected countries.

Japan's 9.9 quake alert that wasn't

Japan's weather agency sent an emergency alert for a magnitude 9.9 quake on Oct 16, then withdrew it about eight minutes later. The real quake was a 6.9 near Kamchatka. JMA apologized, explained the error and denied any cyberattack.
