The quick version

Flash flooding swept across the Four Corners on October 22 and 23, 2026. In Pagosa Springs, Colorado, the San Juan River peaked just over 15 feet around 5:30 a.m. Friday and then began to fall, the Pagosa Springs Sun reports, but mandatory evacuations stayed in place for flood-prone areas. Arizona saw helicopter rescues, southern Utah is cleaning up, and forecasters warn more rain could fall this weekend.

Colorado

The Town of Pagosa Springs says floodwaters may contain untreated sewage after three wastewater lift stations and one pump station were inundated, so stay out of the water. Hot Springs Boulevard is closed at the bridge, and Town Hall, the Community Center and the Visitor Center are closed through the weekend. The Colorado Department of Transportation is urging drivers to avoid travel in the flood watch areas and never to drive around closure gates. Check COtrip.org before you go anywhere.

Utah, Arizona and New Mexico

In Hurricane, Utah, a shipping container drifted down a street before the cleanup began, AP reports, and flood watches shifted to southeast Utah into the weekend. In Arizona, four people were lifted off the roof of a vehicle by helicopter south of Sedona, NBC News reported, and northern New Mexico saw evacuations and roof rescues.

Stay safe

  1. Turn around, don't drown. Just a little moving water can sweep a car away.
  2. Sign up for local alerts (Nixle, your county's emergency page) and follow evacuation orders.
  3. Stay away from riverbanks even as water drops, because the ground is saturated.

Quick answers

Where is the flooding in the Four Corners?

Southwest and south-central Colorado (including Pagosa Springs), southern Utah, northern Arizona and northern New Mexico, on October 22 and 23, 2026.

How high did the San Juan River get in Pagosa Springs?

It peaked just over 15 feet at the Pagosa Springs gauge around 5:30 a.m. on Friday, October 23, then started to recede, per the Pagosa Springs Sun.

Is Wolf Creek Pass open?

CDOT listed US 160 over Wolf Creek Pass as closed as of 8 a.m. on October 23. Check COtrip.org for the latest.

Will it rain more in the Four Corners this weekend?

Forecasters say more heavy rain is possible this weekend, and flood watches were in effect in southeast Utah, per AP.

How do I get flood alerts in Archuleta County?

Text 81147 to 888777 to sign up for Nixle alerts, the county's main emergency channel.
