Rays vs. Brewers: your World Series 2026 cheat sheet

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-10-20

Mark your calendar: the Tampa Bay Rays and the Milwaukee Brewers meet in the 2026 World Series, and here's the fun part. Neither franchise has ever won it. One fan base is about to party like never before.

What it is

The best-of-seven World Series starts Friday, October 23, at 8 p.m. ET at American Family Field in Milwaukee, on FOX. The Brewers host Games 1 and 2, Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg hosts Games 3 to 5, and Milwaukee gets Games 6 and 7 if the series goes that far. MLB.com now lists Nick Martinez for Tampa Bay against Jacob Misiorowski for Milwaukee in Game 1.

Game 1Fri Oct 23, 8 p.m. ET, Milwaukee (FOX)
Game 2Sat Oct 24, 8 p.m. ET, Milwaukee
Game 3Mon Oct 26, 8 p.m. ET, St. Petersburg
Game 7 (if needed)Sat Oct 31, 8 p.m. ET, Milwaukee

Why it's trending

"Rays" sat near the top of X's US trends overnight after Tampa Bay beat Cleveland 7-3 in Game 6 to win the ALCS 4-2. Freddy Peralta handled the Guardians for 5 1/3 innings, Junior Caminero went deep in the seventh and Díaz crushed a 416-foot homer in the eighth. Milwaukee got here by sweeping the two-time defending champion Dodgers, so both teams arrive red-hot. Add two long title droughts (the Brewers' only trip was 1982, the Rays lost in 2008 and 2020) and you've got a story that sells itself. Our World Series 2026 guide has the full schedule and FAQ.

Where to follow it

Every game is on FOX, with FOX Deportes in Spanish and streaming on FOX One. MLB.com has live box scores, and the teams' feeds are the place for clubhouse celebrations. On social, follow #WorldSeries, #RaysUp and #ThisIsMyCrew. Catch up with our ALCS recap, then switch sports: the WNBA Finals are tied 1-1 and the NBA season tips off tonight.

Quick answers

Who is in the 2026 World Series?

The Tampa Bay Rays and the Milwaukee Brewers. Neither team has won a World Series before.

When does the 2026 World Series start?

Game 1 is Friday, October 23, 2026, at 8 p.m. ET at American Family Field in Milwaukee.

What channel is the World Series on?

FOX, with FOX Deportes in Spanish and streaming on FOX One.

When is Game 7 of the World Series?

If needed, Saturday, October 31, 2026, at 8 p.m. ET in Milwaukee.

Who is pitching Game 1 of the World Series?

Nick Martinez for Tampa Bay and Jacob Misiorowski for Milwaukee, per MLB.com's Game 1 preview.
