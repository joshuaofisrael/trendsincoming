iPhone Duo launch week: is Apple's $1,999 foldable worth it?

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-10-19

The phone people have been joking about for a decade is finally real. iPhone Duo, Apple's first foldable, lands in stores Friday, October 23, and it's already the most argued-about gadget of the fall. Here's the friendly rundown before you hit "Buy."

What it is

Closed, it's a compact phone with a 5.4-inch outer screen. Open it like a book and you get a 7.6-inch inner display, which Apple says is 50 percent larger than the iPhone 18 Pro Max's. Inside is the same A20 Pro chip as the 18 Pro, there's a 48MP main camera and a 48MP ultra wide, and it's rated IP68. Colors are Star White and Night Sky.

Why it's trending

Two words: the price. It's $800 more than an iPhone 18 Pro with the same storage, and MacRumors found almost 40 things you give up, including Face ID (it uses Touch ID), the Action button and a dedicated telephoto camera. On the flip side, early hands-ons from Tom's Guide and Bloomberg were glowing about the hinge, the barely-there crease and how good iOS feels on the big screen. So the internet is split, and that's exactly what keeps a launch trending. Pre-order wait times are already long, per MacRumors. Our iPhone Duo guide has every spec and FAQ.

Should you buy one?

Our take: buy it if you'll unfold it every day, for reading, two apps side by side, photo editing or movies on the go. Skip it if you mostly use your phone closed or love zoom shots. Either way, try one in a store, closed and open, before you commit. Trade-in credit runs $175 to $885 for an iPhone 13 or newer.

Where to follow it

Apple Newsroom has the official details, and MacRumors and Tom's Guide will have full reviews and launch-day reports. On social, the #iPhoneDuo tag will be packed with unboxings by Friday. More tech lives on our Tech & AI hub, including the AI model race, and the earnings calendar covers the week's big reports.

Quick answers

When can I buy the iPhone Duo?

It goes on sale Friday, October 23, 2026. Pre-orders opened October 16.

How much does the iPhone Duo cost?

From $1,999 for 256GB up to $3,199 for 2TB in the U.S.

What do you give up with the iPhone Duo?

Compared with the iPhone 18 Pro, it has no Face ID, no Action button and no dedicated telephoto camera, per MacRumors.

Is the iPhone Duo worth it?

It's best for people who will use the big inner screen every day. If you mostly use your phone closed, the iPhone 18 Pro costs less.
