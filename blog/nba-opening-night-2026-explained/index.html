NBA opening night 2026: the Sixers spoil the Knicks' party, and what it means

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-10-21

Basketball is back, and opening night delivered. The Knicks raised a championship banner, handed out rings, and then watched the brand-new Philadelphia 76ers walk out of Madison Square Garden with a 106-100 win.

What it is

The NBA opened its 2026-27 season with a first-ever opening day tripleheader on NBC and Peacock. Here's the scoreboard:

Pistons 121, Celtics 113Detroit rallied from 22 points down
76ers 106, Knicks 100Maxey 28, Brown 19, LeBron 14-9-7
Thunder 116, Spurs 104A West finals rematch goes to OKC

Why it's trending

Because the Sixers spent the summer going all in. They traded for Jaylen Brown and signed LeBron James away from the Lakers, and night one showed why. Tyrese Maxey led everyone with 28, Brown added 19 with eight rebounds, and LeBron, starting his NBA-record 24th season, chipped in 14 points, nine rebounds and seven assists per the official box score. A Brown three at the third-quarter buzzer capped an 11-0 run, and Joel Embiid's late three answered the Knicks every time they got close. Meanwhile, Jalen Brunson scored 21 but shot 6 for 19. Our NBA page has the full opening night recap.

Where to follow it

Games are spread across NBC and Peacock, ESPN and ABC, and Prime Video this season, so check our where-to-watch guide. On social, follow #NBA, #Sixers and #Knicks. Not done with basketball tonight? The WNBA Finals Game 3 tips at 8 p.m. ET, and the World Series starts Friday.

Quick answers

Who won the Knicks vs. 76ers opening night game?

The 76ers won 106-100 at Madison Square Garden on October 20, 2026.

How did LeBron James do in his 76ers debut?

He had 14 points, 9 rebounds and 7 assists in Philadelphia's 106-100 win, per the NBA's official box score.

Who won Celtics vs. Pistons?

Detroit, 121-113, after rallying from a 22-point deficit.
