You Can See Everything: Nathan Fielder's Elizabeth Holmes movie, explained (no spoilers)

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-10-16

Update, Oct 18: Opening day was huge for a documentary: $5.5M on Friday, previews included, in just 855 theaters, per Deadline. Deadline now projects $12M to $14M for the weekend, the second-best documentary opening ever behind Fahrenheit 9/11. We're tracking it on our box office page.

Nathan Fielder made a documentary about Elizabeth Holmes. If you've seen The Rehearsal, that sentence probably made you sit up. You Can See Everything, from A24, opens in US theaters today, Friday, October 16, and it's the rare documentary people are treating like an event movie.

What it is

A24's official description sets it up simply: thirty-four days before she goes to prison, the Theranos founder invites a skeptical film crew to document her every move. What starts as an intimate portrait turns into what A24 calls a "mind-bending three-year journey." Fielder co-directed with Lance Oppenheim, and Holmes's partner Billy Evans is part of the story too.

Quick background if you need it: Holmes founded Theranos, the blood-testing startup that collapsed, and she was later convicted of defrauding investors and sentenced to federal prison. In the trailer, she still says she did nothing wrong. The film puts her in front of Fielder's famously deadpan camera, and Deadline's review said it uncovers "one zinger after another."

Why it's trending

Secrecy, mostly. It premiered as a mystery screening at the Telluride Film Festival on September 6, where audiences had to hand over their phones, and the directors asked everyone not to spoil the twists. IndieWire says the film was made in secret over three years, and awards watchers immediately started talking about it as a real Best Picture threat. No documentary has ever landed a Best Picture nomination.

It's also doing real business for a doc. IndieWire reports it's opening in about 800 theaters and projecting a debut of up to $8 million, and Deadline says it earned about $2 million in Thursday previews. For a nearly three-hour documentary, that's huge.

The quick facts

  • Directors: Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim
  • Studio: A24
  • Runtime: 2 hr 55 min, per A24
  • Where: in theaters now. No streaming date has been announced.

How to see it without getting spoiled

  1. Go this weekend if you can. The longer you wait, the harder it gets to dodge the twists.
  2. Mute the title and the hashtag on X and TikTok for a few days.
  3. Skip the comment sections on review posts. That's where spoilers hide.
  4. Grab a snack. It's long.

What's next

Weekend box office numbers land Sunday, and we'll track them on our box office page. It's going head to head with Street Fighter, which is about as different a movie night as you can get. Awards season chatter is just getting started.
