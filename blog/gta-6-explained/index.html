GTA VI is five weeks away. Here's everything that's actually confirmed.

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-10-14

Let's get the big one out of the way: Grand Theft Auto VI launches Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Standard edition, $79.99. If you pre-ordered digitally, you can start preloading on November 12. That's not a rumor or a leak. It's on Rockstar's own site, the Xbox store and Take-Two's press release.

So what is it, exactly?

GTA VI is Rockstar's first new mainline Grand Theft Auto since GTA V in 2013. You heard that right. A whole console generation and then some. This time we're back in Vice City, which sits inside a fictional state called Leonida. Basically, it's Rockstar's spin on Florida. The story follows two leads, Jason and Lucia. Rockstar's official pitch: an easy score goes wrong, and they end up "on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America," caught in a conspiracy that stretches across the state.

It's a single-player story first. Rockstar's press release says it "features a single-player experience set in the biggest, most immersive evolution of the series yet." Online plans? Rockstar hasn't laid them out in the materials we checked, so we're not going to guess.

Why it's trending right now

Three reasons. First, the countdown is finally short enough to feel real. Five weeks. Second, November is stacked. Nintendo's Ocarina of Time remake lands November 5, two weeks before GTA VI, and Game Informer pointed out that the remake is launching "just two weeks before Grand Theft Auto VI, which is anticipated to be the biggest game launch of the year." People are doing the November budget math out loud online.

Third, preload. Rockstar confirmed digital pre-orders can preload from November 12, and physical copies, which contain a download code, also go on sale November 12 so people can preload. So "is the disc just a code?" has become its own mini-discourse. Short answer: per Rockstar, yes, the physical version contains a download code.

The backstory (it's a long one)

GTA VI has been a slow burn. It was originally aimed at fall 2025. In May 2025, Rockstar moved it to May 26, 2026. Then, on November 6, 2025, alongside Take-Two's quarterly earnings, it slid again, this time to November 19, 2026. Rockstar's statement at the time: "We are sorry for adding additional time to what we realize has been a long wait, but these extra months will allow us to finish the game with the level of polish you have come to expect and deserve."

Pre-orders opened June 25, 2026, at midnight local time. Since then, the date has held. Our honest take: after two delays, the fact that Rockstar is now talking preload dates is the strongest signal yet that November 19 is locked.

What we know vs. what we don't

  • Confirmed: Nov 19 launch, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, $79.99 standard, Nov 12 preload, Vice City in Leonida, Jason and Lucia.
  • Not announced: a PC version or date. Rockstar's launch platforms are console only. History says PC tends to come later for Rockstar games, but that's history, not an announcement.
  • Ultimate Edition: Rockstar's site lists one, described as "an exclusive collection of items threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia's story." Pre-order bonus: the Vintage Vice City Pack.

What's next

November 12: preload opens and physical copies arrive. Clear your SSD space now. Seriously, do it this weekend. November 19: launch. Expect servers, stores and your group chat to be very busy. Between now and then, watch Rockstar's official channels for any final trailer or launch details. That's where real news shows up first.

Where to follow it

Stick to official sources for dates: Rockstar's GTA VI page, the PlayStation and Xbox store listings, and Take-Two's investor releases. For the vibes, memes and wild theories, the hashtag links below will take you straight into the conversation. Just remember: a theory with a lot of likes is still a theory.

Quick answers

When does GTA VI come out?

Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, per Rockstar.

Can I preload GTA VI?

Yes. Digital pre-orders can preload from November 12, 2026, and physical copies with a download code also go on sale that day.

Is GTA VI on PC?

Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version or date.
