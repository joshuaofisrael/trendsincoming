The company behind Claude wants to go public, and Wall Street noticed. Here's the quick, no-hype version.

What it is

On October 26, 2026, Anthropic filed a Form S-1 with the SEC to list its Class A shares on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol "ANT." The share count and price range haven't been set.

TickerANT (Nasdaq)
Revenue, six months to June$16.29B, vs. $1.16B a year earlier (CNBC)
Voting controlFounders 50.1% via a Founder LLC (CNBC)
IPO dateNot set

Why it's trending

It moved the market. The Nasdaq rose 0.82% Monday and chip stocks rallied, CNBC reports, with Nvidia up 2.4%. "This is a big confidence booster," Jay Woods of Freedom Capital Markets told CNBC.

Quick answers

When is the Anthropic IPO?

No date is set. Anthropic filed its S-1 on October 26, 2026, and the price range and share count have not been determined.

What is Anthropic's stock ticker?

"ANT" on the Nasdaq Global Select Market, per Anthropic.

Can I buy Anthropic stock now?

No. The registration isn't effective yet, so the shares can't be sold until the IPO prices.
