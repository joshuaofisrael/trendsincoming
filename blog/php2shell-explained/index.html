PHP2Shell explained: why websites went dark to patch this week

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-10-23

If a few of your favorite sites blinked out this week, here's probably why. Two serious bugs turned up in PHP, the language quietly running a huge share of the web, and site owners everywhere scrambled to patch.

What it is

The flaws are tracked as CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, and researchers nicknamed them PHP2Shell. In plain English, they could let a stranger run their own code on a web server without a password. On October 21, the U.S. cybersecurity agency CISA told everyone to update to PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 or 8.2.35 right away, and said PHP 8.1 and older have no fix at all.

Fixed versions8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36, 8.2.35
PHP 8.1 or olderNo patch; take it offline
Found byCalif and Wiz researchers, using AI
Exploited?Not seen by CISA at publication; Cloudflare's CTO told the BBC early attacks were emerging

Why it's trending

Three things made this huge. The bugs sat unnoticed for more than a decade. Details leaked on social media before the fix was ready, which forced an early announcement. And the researchers found them with AI, through OpenAI's Patch the Planet program, per the BBC. Wiz called them the "most severe vulnerabilities ever discovered" by AI. Then the outages started: Finnish government services, the Dutch cyber agency's own site and some Wikimedia systems all went offline while teams patched.

Where to follow it

CISA's alert and PHP.net are the official sources. If you run a site, ask your host whether it's patched. On social, #PHP and #infosec are where developers are trading notes. Our PHP2Shell guide has the step-by-step checklist, and you can catch up on the Google Cloud outage that took Revolut down, open-weight AI models and everything else in tech and AI.

Quick answers

What is PHP2Shell?

The nickname for two critical PHP vulnerabilities, CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, that allow unauthenticated remote code execution on PHP installations serving web requests.

Which PHP versions fix PHP2Shell?

PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 and 8.2.35, per CISA's October 21, 2026 alert.

Is PHP 8.1 affected?

Yes, and CISA says PHP 8.1 and earlier have no fix, so they should be taken offline.

Is PHP2Shell being exploited?

CISA had not observed exploitation when it published. Cloudflare's CTO told the BBC early attacks were emerging.
