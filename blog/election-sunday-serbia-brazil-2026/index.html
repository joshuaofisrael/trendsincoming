Election Sunday: Serbia and Brazil vote on the same day

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-10-25

Nonpartisan explainer: sourced facts, both sides represented, no endorsements.

Two countries, two very different races, one Sunday. Here's the quick, no-spin version of both.

What it is

Brazil is holding a presidential runoff between incumbent Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva and Sen. Flavio Bolsonaro. Serbia is holding a snap parliamentary election for 250 seats, with Aleksandar Vucic's SNS facing the Student List led by Ilija Srdanovic.

BrazilRunoff today; polls neck and neck; official results from the TSE
SerbiaPolls 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. local (06:00 to 19:00 GMT); first indications about 2 hours later

Why it's trending

Both are close. In Brazil, a Saturday CNT/MDA poll gave Bolsonaro a 1.3-point edge, Reuters reports, and Minas Gerais is the state to watch: nobody has won the presidency without it since the 1980s. In Serbia, the vote follows nearly two years of student-led protests that began after the 2024 Novi Sad canopy collapse killed 16. Polls there disagree, with Ipsos showing SNS at 47% and the students at 40% while other surveys had the students ahead, NBC News and Reuters report.

Quick answers

When is the Brazil runoff?

Today, Sunday, October 25, 2026.

Who is in Brazil's presidential runoff?

President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva of the Workers' Party and Sen. Flavio Bolsonaro, the eldest son of former President Jair Bolsonaro.

When is the Serbia election?

Today, Sunday, October 25, 2026. Polls are open from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. local time (06:00 to 19:00 GMT).

Who is running in the Serbia election?

18 lists are on the ballot. The main contest is Aleksandar Vucic's Serbian Progressive Party (SNS) against the Student List led by Ilija Srdanovic.
