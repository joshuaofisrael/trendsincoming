Is there a government shutdown in October 2026? No. The deadline is Dec 11

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-10-14

Nonpartisan explainer: sourced facts, both sides represented, no endorsements.

Short answer: there is no government shutdown in October 2026. The current government funding deadline is Friday, December 11, 2026. The stopgap law H.R. 6500 (Public Law 119-103), signed September 2, funds federal agencies through that date, per Congress.gov (checked October 24, 2026). If Congress enacts no new funding by the end of December 11, a shutdown could start Saturday, December 12.

Correction, Oct 17, 2026: An earlier version of this page said Congress faced an October 31 deadline to fund the government. That was wrong, and we should have checked it against the law itself. The stopgap funding law signed on September 2 runs through December 11, 2026. We've rewritten this guide and corrected our trend card. Sorry about that.

The next real fight over government funding happens after the November 3 election, in the lame-duck session. Here is a calm, nonpartisan rundown of where things stand, the dates to know and what a shutdown would mean for you. Want just the dates? See our government shutdown deadline 2026 page.

What it is

Federal agencies run on money Congress approves through 12 yearly appropriations bills. When those aren't done by the October 1 start of the fiscal year, Congress usually passes a short-term extension called a continuing resolution, or CR, that keeps funding flowing at roughly last year's levels. If neither happens by the deadline, agencies without funding stop non-essential work. That's a shutdown.

This year Congress passed H.R. 6500, the Continuing Appropriations and Extensions Act, 2027, and the president signed it on September 2, 2026. Per the American Presidency Project's record of the signing, it funds federal agencies through December 11, 2026. Holland & Knight notes it also extends surface transportation programs and the National Flood Insurance Program to the same date.

Why it's trending

"Shutdown" is one of those words that trends whenever anyone says it near a calendar. With the midterms close and Congress out of town, plenty of posts (and, yes, at least one report we relied on) pointed to an October 31 date. The law says otherwise. If you see a Halloween shutdown claim, check it against the bill on Congress.gov.

Where things actually stand

  • Funding: in place through Dec 11, 2026.
  • Progress on full-year bills: according to Holland & Knight's Oct 9 analysis, the House has passed 3 of the 12 fiscal 2027 appropriations bills, and the Senate had not passed any before its pre-election recess.
  • Options in December: a big package (an omnibus or several "minibuses"), another CR into early 2027, or a mix. A lapse for some agencies is possible if talks stall.
  • Likely sticking points: defense versus non-defense spending, Homeland Security and border enforcement funding, disaster aid, and policy riders. Both parties have their own priorities on each, and we'll link their arguments rather than pick a side.
  • Signals: House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries (D) has said he favors finishing the appropriations bills in the lame duck, per Holland & Knight, which also sees a second CR as a realistic outcome.

The backstory

There's a bigger argument running in the background about who controls spending. Holland & Knight notes the administration's ability to transfer, withhold, rescind or condition money Congress approved could become a major negotiating issue, and Federal News Network has covered the debate over Congress's power of the purse. Supporters of broader executive flexibility and defenders of congressional control both make their case there.

Update, Saturday, October 24: no change. The deadline is still December 11, which is 48 days away, and Congress is out campaigning until after the November 3 election. Also on our radar: the midterms, 10 days out and early voting by state.

What a shutdown would mean for you

  • Social Security and SSI: payments continue on their normal dates, the Social Security Administration says. Local offices stay open with reduced services.
  • Mail: keeps coming. The Postal Service generally receives no tax dollars for operating expenses and funds itself through postage and services.
  • National parks: depends on the National Park Service contingency plan. In past shutdowns, some parks stayed accessible with limited or no services while visitor centers closed. Check nps.gov before a trip.
  • Federal workers: employees whose work is not excepted are furloughed; excepted employees keep working, per OPM guidance.

Quick answers

Is there a government shutdown in October 2026?

No. The Continuing Appropriations and Extensions Act, 2027 (H.R. 6500, Public Law 119-103), signed September 2, 2026, funds federal agencies through December 11, 2026. There is no October deadline.

Is there a government shutdown on October 31, 2026?

No. Some posts pointed to an October 31 deadline, but the law on Congress.gov funds the government through December 11, 2026.

When is the next government funding deadline?

Friday, December 11, 2026. If no new funding law is enacted by the end of that day, agencies without appropriations would start shutting down on Saturday, December 12.

Could there be a government shutdown in December 2026?

It is possible, not certain. By December 11, Congress could pass full-year funding, another short-term extension into 2027, or a mix of the two. A shutdown happens only if no funding law is enacted in time.

Will Social Security payments stop in a government shutdown?

No. The Social Security Administration says Social Security and SSI payments continue on their normal dates during a shutdown. Local offices stay open with reduced services, and some requests, like proof of benefits letters, wait until it ends.

Does the mail still come during a government shutdown?

Yes. The Postal Service generally receives no tax dollars for operating expenses and pays its way through postage and services, so mail delivery continues.

Are national parks open during a government shutdown?

It depends on the National Park Service contingency plan in effect. In past shutdowns, some parks stayed accessible with limited or no visitor services, while staffed facilities such as visitor centers closed. Check nps.gov and the Interior Department's shutdown page before you go.

What happens to federal workers in a shutdown?

Employees whose work is not excepted are furloughed. Excepted employees, such as those protecting life and property, keep working. OPM's furlough guidance has the details.

How many days until the government shutdown deadline?

Funding runs out at the end of Friday, December 11, 2026. Our homepage countdown shows the time left, and our shutdown page tracks any change Congress makes.

Do members of the military get paid during a government shutdown?

Active-duty troops keep working during a lapse. In past shutdowns, paychecks could be delayed unless Congress passed a separate pay measure or the lapse ended before payday. Watch for any pay bill in the lame-duck session.

Do TSA and air traffic controllers work during a shutdown?

Yes. They are excepted employees who keep working, but in past shutdowns they went without pay until funding was restored, which can mean longer security lines if staff call out.

Has Congress done anything about the December 11 deadline yet?

Not as of October 21, 2026. Congress is out until after the November 3 election, so action is expected in the lame-duck session in November and December.

Who decides whether there is a shutdown?

Congress must pass funding bills and the President must sign them, or Congress must override a veto. If no funding law is enacted by the deadline, agencies without appropriations begin an orderly shutdown.

How many days until the government shutdown deadline?

As of October 24, 2026, the funding deadline of Friday, December 11 is 48 days away. A lapse could begin on Saturday, December 12 if no new funding law is enacted.

Has anything changed with the December 11 deadline?

Not as of October 24, 2026. Congress.gov still shows H.R. 6500 (Public Law 119-103) funding the government through December 11, and Congress is not expected back until after the November 3 election.

Will the government shut down after the midterms?

Only if Congress and the President do not enact new funding by the end of Friday, December 11, 2026. Lawmakers return after the November 3 election with about five weeks to pass full-year bills or another stopgap.

What day would a government shutdown start in December 2026?

Saturday, December 12, 2026, if no new funding law is enacted by the December 11 deadline set by H.R. 6500 (Public Law 119-103).
