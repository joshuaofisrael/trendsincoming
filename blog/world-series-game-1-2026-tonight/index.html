World Series Game 1 tonight: Rays vs. Brewers cheat sheet

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-10-23

Grab a seat: the World Series starts tonight, and whoever wins, a city celebrates its first-ever championship.

What it is

The Tampa Bay Rays visit the Milwaukee Brewers for Game 1 on Friday, October 23, at 8 p.m. ET at American Family Field, on FOX. MLB.com lists Nick Martinez for the Rays against Jacob Misiorowski, who threw 1,115 pitches of 100 mph or more this season.

Game 1Tonight, 8 p.m. ET, Milwaukee (FOX)
Game 2Sat Oct 24, Milwaukee
Games 3 to 5From Mon Oct 26, St. Petersburg
Games 6 and 7Oct 30 and 31 in Milwaukee, if needed

Why it's trending

It's the first World Series between two franchises that have never won it since 1980, per AP. Milwaukee won 103 games and hasn't been here since 1982, while Tampa Bay won 97 and is chasing the title it missed in 2008 and 2020. Christian Yelich summed up the mood to MLB.com: "our performance directly affects people's happiness and togetherness."

Where to follow it

Watch on FOX, FOX Deportes or FOX One, and follow #WorldSeries, #ThisIsMyCrew and #RaysUp. Our World Series 2026 guide has the full schedule, and the series preview covers both rosters. When is Game 7 of the World Series?

Who is pitching Game 1 of the World Series?
