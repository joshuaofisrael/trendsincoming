Brewers win the World Series: the title in one minute

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-10-30

Milwaukee, it finally happened. Here's the quick version.

What happened

Game 5Brewers 4, Rays 1 (Oct 28)
SeriesBrewers win 4-1
MVPWilliam Contreras
ParadeToday, Fri Oct 30, 10 a.m. CT

Why it's trending

It's the first World Series title in Brewers history, MLB.com reports. Contreras went 8-for-21 with four RBIs and threw out four of nine base stealers. "I don't have words to say how I'm feeling," he said, per MLB.com.

Where to follow it

The parade starts at N. 4th St. and W. Wisconsin Ave. and streams free on Brewers.com, per MLB.com. Follow #ThisIsMyCrew. Our champions guide has the route and rally, and WNBA Finals Game 7 is Saturday.

Quick answers

Who won the 2026 World Series?

The Milwaukee Brewers, 4 games to 1 over the Tampa Bay Rays. They clinched with a 4-1 win in Game 5 on October 28, 2026.

Is this the Brewers' first World Series title?

Yes. MLB.com calls it the first World Series title in franchise history.

Who was the 2026 World Series MVP?

Brewers catcher William Contreras. He went 8-for-21 with four RBIs and threw out four of nine would-be base stealers, per MLB.com.

What time is the Brewers parade?

10 a.m. CT on Friday, October 30, 2026, starting at N. 4th St. and W. Wisconsin Ave. and ending with a free rally at Henry Maier Festival Park.
