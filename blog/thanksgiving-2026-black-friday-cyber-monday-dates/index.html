Thanksgiving 2026 date: Black Friday, Cyber Monday and the whole week

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-11-09

Short answer: Thanksgiving 2026 is Thursday, November 26, per OPM's federal holiday schedule. By law, Thanksgiving Day is the fourth Thursday in November (5 U.S.C. 6103). That puts Black Friday on November 27, Small Business Saturday on November 28, Cyber Monday on November 30 and Giving Tuesday on December 1. Next year, Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 25, 2027.

Thanksgiving week 2026 at a glance

DayDateWhat it is
Thanksgiving DayThu Nov 26Federal holiday
Black FridayFri Nov 27Retail sales day; not a federal holiday
Small Business SaturdaySat Nov 28Shop-local day
Cyber MondayMon Nov 30Online sales day
Giving TuesdayTue Dec 1Charity giving day

Black Friday, Small Business Saturday, Cyber Monday and Giving Tuesday are not official holidays. They are retail and charity traditions that always land on the days after Thanksgiving, which is why their dates move each year with it.

Is Black Friday a day off?

Not for federal employees: OPM lists Thanksgiving Day, not the Friday after, as the federal holiday. Many state governments, schools and private employers do give the Friday off, so check your own calendar.

Why the date changes

Thanksgiving is pinned to the fourth Thursday in November rather than a fixed date, so it can land anywhere from November 22 to November 28. In 2026 it falls on the 26th, which leaves a short four-week run from Black Friday to Christmas on Friday, December 25.
