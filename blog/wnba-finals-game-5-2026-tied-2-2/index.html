WNBA Finals Game 5: tied 2-2, and it's back to San Francisco

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-10-25

Just when it looked like Atlanta was cruising, Golden State flipped the script. This series is now a best-of-three.

What it is

The Golden State Valkyries won Game 4 in Atlanta, 67-62, to tie the WNBA Finals 2-2. Game 5 is Monday, October 26, at 8 p.m. ET (5 p.m. PT) at Chase Center in San Francisco, on USA.

Why it's trending

Defense. Golden State led by 16 at the half and held Atlanta to 33.3% shooting and 4 of 23 from three, per AP. Gabby Williams scored 17, Kiah Stokes grabbed 16 rebounds with three blocks, and Tiffany Hayes scored all 13 of her points after halftime. Angel Reese was held to 8 points on 2 of 12 shooting. And it's a big deal either way: neither franchise has ever won a title.
