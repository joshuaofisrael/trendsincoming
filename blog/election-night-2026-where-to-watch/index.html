Election night 2026: where to watch, in one minute

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-10-31

Nonpartisan explainer: sourced facts, both sides represented, no endorsements.

Snacks ready? Here's how to follow election night, whoever you're rooting for.

What it is

WhenTue Nov 3, 2026
EarliestCNN, C-SPAN: 4 p.m. ET
Free streamPeacock (portions of NBC) from 5 p.m. ET
BroadcastABC and CBS 7 p.m. ET; NBC 5 p.m. ET

Why it's trending

All 435 House seats, 35 Senate races and 36 governor races are on the ballot. Networks have rolled out their plans, from NBC's Decision '26, running 5 p.m. to 6 a.m. ET, to C-SPAN's commercial-free results, per TV Insider. Remember that election night numbers are unofficial until states certify.

Where to follow it

Follow #ElectionNight. Our full network-by-network guide has every start time, and Elections 2026 explains what's on the ballot. Vote first: USA.gov links your state's election office.

Quick answers

What time does election night coverage start in 2026?

CNN and C-SPAN start at 4 p.m. ET on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. NBC starts at 5 p.m. ET, Fox News and MS Now at 6 p.m. ET, and ABC, CBS and NewsNation at 7 p.m. ET, per TV Insider.

Can I stream election night for free?

Per TV Insider, portions of NBC's coverage stream on Peacock from 5 p.m. ET, and CBS News runs a show on its digital platforms from 5 p.m. ET. C-SPAN carries commercial-free results.

When do polls close on November 3, 2026?

Each state sets its own hours. Check your state election office, linked from USA.gov.
