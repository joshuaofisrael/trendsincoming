What it is

The National League Championship Series is the best-of-seven that decides who represents the NL in the World Series. This year it's Los Angeles Dodgers vs. Milwaukee Brewers. Over in the ALCS, it's Rays vs. Guardians. The World Series starts October 23.

Why it's trending

October baseball always travels, but this matchup has the perfect mix: a blue-blood Dodgers clubhouse, a Brewers team that refuses to be a footnote, star pitching names in the trend list, and managers who make for great second-screen debate.
