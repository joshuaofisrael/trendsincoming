Valkyries vs. Dream: why the 2026 WNBA Finals are a must-watch

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-10-17

Somebody is about to win their first WNBA title, and either way it's going to be a great story. The Golden State Valkyries host the Atlanta Dream in Game 1 of the WNBA Finals today, Saturday, October 17, at 3:30 p.m. ET (12:30 p.m. PT) on NBC and Peacock. Neither team has ever won it all.

What it is

The WNBA Finals is a best-of-seven series. Per WNBA.com, this is only the eighth time in the league's 30 years that both finalists are chasing a first championship. Games 1, 2, 5 and 7 are at Chase Center in San Francisco, Games 3 and 4 are at State Farm Arena in Atlanta, and the venue for Atlanta's Game 6, if needed, is still to be set.

*If needed. Times and channels per WNBA.com; check the morning of each game.

Why it's trending

The Valkyries are in year two. Last season they became the first expansion team to make the playoffs, as a No. 8 seed. This season they went 32-12, finished No. 2 with the league's top-ranked defense, rallied from 16 down to win a Game 3 against Dallas, then swept the defending champion Las Vegas Aces. Coach Natalie Nakase beat her old boss, Becky Hammon, along the way.

The Dream are on a heater. Atlanta went a franchise-best 30-14, hasn't lost a playoff game (2-0 over Washington, 3-0 over New York) and rides a 10-game winning streak. Its All-Star trio is Allisha Gray, Rhyne Howard and Angel Reese, who was just named Defensive Player of the Year. Atlanta has reached the Finals three times before (2010, 2011 and 2013) and was swept each time, so this is a chance to rewrite that.

Add a big Bay Area crowd and two fan bases who've never celebrated a title, and you can see why it's all over the timeline.

What to watch

  • Defense on defense. Reese won DPOY and Golden State's Gabby Williams was runner-up. The Valkyries were the league's best regular-season defense; in the playoffs, Atlanta has the top defensive rating.
  • Stars vs. depth. Atlanta leaned on its starters more than any team. Golden State led the league in bench scoring, with Sixth Player of the Year Janelle Salaun.
  • Allisha Gray's hand. Atlanta's leading scorer missed the last two semifinal games after a left-hand injury and a minor procedure. She called herself day-to-day this week.
  • The season series. Golden State won all three regular-season meetings, but the last one was July 4, before Atlanta added two-time champion DeWanna Bonner.

Where to follow it

WNBA.com and ESPN for scores and the official schedule, NBC and Peacock for Game 1, and the hashtag links below for the party. We keep our WNBA Finals 2026 hub updated all series. It's a big sports week overall: the World Series starts Oct 23, the NBA tips off Tuesday, and the NBA Cup starts Oct 30.

Quick answers

When is Game 1 of the 2026 WNBA Finals?

Saturday, October 17, 2026, at 3:30 p.m. ET at Chase Center in San Francisco.

What channel is the WNBA Finals on?

Game 1 is on NBC and Peacock. Games 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7 are on USA, Peacock and CNBC, and Game 4 is on NBC and Peacock, per WNBA.com.

Who is in the 2026 WNBA Finals?

The No. 2 seed Golden State Valkyries and the No. 4 seed Atlanta Dream. Neither franchise has won a WNBA title.

Who won the regular-season series?

Golden State won all three meetings, most recently on July 4.
