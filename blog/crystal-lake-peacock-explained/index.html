Crystal Lake on Peacock: why everyone's binging the Friday the 13th prequel

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-10-18

Spooky season just got its binge. Crystal Lake, Peacock's Friday the 13th prequel series, dropped all eight episodes on October 15, and the whole internet seems to be somewhere between episode three and "okay, one more." Here's what it is, why it's everywhere this weekend, and how to watch it tonight.

What it is

Crystal Lake is a Peacock and A24 series from showrunner Brad Caleb Kane, who also worked on It: Welcome to Derry. It goes back before the 1980 movie and centers on Pam Voorhees, played by Linda Cardellini, the camp cook whose grief over her drowned son turns into something much scarier. Callum Vinson plays young Jason. The story opens in 1972 at Camp Crystal Lake, per Variety, and it leans more psychological thriller than pure slasher, with plenty of gore for the people who came for that.

Why it's trending

The all-at-once drop. No weekly wait means spoiler season started immediately, and #CrystalLake has been riding X's trending stories all weekend.

The Easter eggs. Fans noticed fast that the episode titles echo the sequels: "A New Beginning," "The New Blood," "Friday..." and "The Final Chapter." Variety calls Episode 7, the 1959 flashback, the best of the season.

The Cardellini factor. Even critics who think the middle drags praise her performance. That split, critics lukewarm and fans hooked, is exactly the kind of thing that keeps a show in the conversation. Our full Crystal Lake guide has the cast, episode list and FAQ.

Where to follow it

Peacock's own blog has the official cast list, and the reaction is loudest on X and TikTok, where people are posting their Pam theories. Planning a whole horror night? Pair it with our Halloween 2026 costume rankings (Pam Voorhees in a cardigan is an easy one), check the weekend box office for what's scary in theaters, and keep an eye on everything trending in TV and streaming. Halloween itself is Saturday, Oct 31, so you have time to finish it twice.

Quick answers

Where can I watch Crystal Lake?

Only on Peacock. All eight episodes premiered on October 15, 2026.

Who plays Pam Voorhees in Crystal Lake?

Linda Cardellini. Callum Vinson plays young Jason.

Is Crystal Lake scary?

It's rated TV-MA and has plenty of gore, but critics describe it as more of a psychological thriller than a pure slasher.

Do I need to see the Friday the 13th movies first?

No. It's a prequel, though fans of the films will catch callbacks like the episode titles.
