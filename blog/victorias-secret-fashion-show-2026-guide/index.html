Victoria's Secret Fashion Show 2026: time, performers and how to watch free

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-10-17

Clear your Sunday night. The Victoria's Secret Fashion Show 2026 streams live from Los Angeles on Sunday, October 18, at 7:30 p.m. ET (4:30 p.m. PT), and it's free. The brand is calling it "Supermodel Sunday," and honestly, the lineup earns the name.

How to watch

  • When: Sunday, Oct 18, 7:30 p.m. ET / 4:30 p.m. PT.
  • Where: free on Victoria's Secret's official YouTube channel, and across its Instagram and TikTok accounts.
  • Cost: nothing. No subscription needed.

Who's performing

An all-women lineup, per Billboard: KATSEYE, Megan Moroney, Megan Thee Stallion and Tate McRae. That's a K-pop-trained global girl group, a country star, a rap superstar and a pop star, all on one runway. KATSEYE and Moroney both scored their first No. 1 albums this year, and McRae arrives with a brand-new single, "Housewife," out Oct 15.

Who's walking

Yahoo reports the confirmed models include Candice Swanepoel, Adriana Lima, Gigi Hadid, Ashley Graham and Alex Consani, among others. Classic Angels next to the internet's favorite new faces is very much the point.

Why it's trending

Two reasons. First, the new Angel reveal. Victoria's Secret ran a docuseries on YouTube, Angels Among Us, directed by Nadia Hallgren, following hopefuls from castings in Chicago, Houston, Los Angeles and Miami. The winner walks the runway on Sunday and gets an exclusive contract with IMG Models. People have been picking favorites for weeks.

Second, the show has a long history of giant music moments. Billboard points out past performers include Taylor Swift (2013 and 2014), Rihanna (2012 and 2015) and Lady Gaga (2016), and last year's all-women lineup was Karol G, TWICE, Missy Elliott and Madison Beer. Fans expect at least one clip from Sunday to live on TikTok for months.

Watch-party ideas

Our take: go pink, pick your top look before the show, have everyone guess the new Angel, and keep a running vote for best performance. Our beauty trends page has the hair and makeup looks to copy (copper hair and smoky eyes are having a moment).

Where to follow it

The Victoria's Secret YouTube channel for the stream, the brand's press room for official details, and the hashtag links below for live reactions. Our show hub has everything in one place.

Quick answers

What time is the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show 2026?

Sunday, October 18, 2026, at 7:30 p.m. ET / 4:30 p.m. PT.

Where can I watch it?

Free on Victoria's Secret's official YouTube channel, plus its Instagram and TikTok accounts.

Who is performing?

KATSEYE, Megan Moroney, Megan Thee Stallion and Tate McRae.

Who is walking?

Confirmed models include Candice Swanepoel, Adriana Lima, Gigi Hadid, Ashley Graham and Alex Consani, plus the winner of the Angels Among Us search.
