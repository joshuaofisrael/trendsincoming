World Series Game 2 tonight: can the Rays even it up?

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-10-24

If you went to bed early last night, you missed a classic finish. Here's your two-minute catch-up before Game 2.

What it is

The Milwaukee Brewers beat the Tampa Bay Rays 3-2 in Game 1 on Friday and lead the best-of-seven World Series 1-0. Game 2 is tonight, Saturday, October 24, at 8 p.m. ET at American Family Field on FOX, with Logan Henderson starting for Milwaukee and Drew Rasmussen for Tampa Bay.

Why it's trending

That ending. With the bases loaded in the ninth, the Brewers thought they'd turned a game-ending double play and poured out of the dugout. Replay said no: shortstop Cooper Pratt's foot was just off the bag, so a run scored and the game kept going. Closer Trevor Megill reset and struck out Jonny DeLuca with two runners in scoring position, AP reports. Jacob Misiorowski got the win, and Christian Yelich's first-inning triple set the tone.

Quick answers

Who won Game 1 of the 2026 World Series?

The Milwaukee Brewers beat the Tampa Bay Rays 3-2 on October 23, 2026, at American Family Field.

When is World Series Game 2?

Saturday, October 24, 2026, at 8 p.m. ET in Milwaukee, on FOX, FOX Deportes and FOX One.

Who is pitching Game 2 of the World Series?

Logan Henderson for Milwaukee and Drew Rasmussen for Tampa Bay, per MLB.com and NBC Sports.

What happened at the end of World Series Game 1?

A game-ending double play was overturned on replay because shortstop Cooper Pratt's foot was off the bag, a run scored, and then Trevor Megill struck out Jonny DeLuca to finish the 3-2 win, AP reports.
