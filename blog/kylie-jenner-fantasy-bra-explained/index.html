Kylie Jenner's $50 million Fantasy Bra: why it broke the internet

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-10-19

Of all the moments at Sunday's Victoria's Secret Fashion Show, one owned the group chats: Kylie Jenner in the Fantasy Bra. It's the first Fantasy Bra on the runway since 2018, and at $50 million, it's the most valuable one ever made. Here's the scoop.

What it is

The Fantasy Bra is Victoria's Secret's once-a-show piece of wearable high jewelry, a tradition that started in 1996 with a $1 million design. This year's was made with jeweler Jacob & Co. and has 4,410 natural diamonds around a 75.54-carat Fancy Vivid yellow diamond. Vogue reports it took 15 craftspeople more than 1,000 hours, weighs just under four pounds, and uses no fabric at all, only gold and diamonds.

Value$50 million
Diamonds4,410, in seven cuts
Centerpiece75.54-carat yellow diamond
Previous record$15 million (Gisele Bündchen, 2000)

Why it's trending

Fans asked for it. VS creative chief Adam Selman told Vogue the Fantasy Bra was one of the most requested things after last year's show, and putting it on someone with Kylie's reach turned it into a pop-culture moment instantly. "Kylie" and "Victoria's Secret" both sat in X's US trends overnight. The other big story from the show: 21-year-old Solange Omon won Angels Among Us and became the newest Angel. Our Fantasy Bra guide has every Fantasy Bra from 1996 to now, and the show recap has the rest.

The full show replay is on Victoria's Secret's YouTube channel, and Vogue and ELLE have the best behind-the-scenes coverage. On social, follow #FantasyBra, #VSFashionShow and #KylieJenner.
