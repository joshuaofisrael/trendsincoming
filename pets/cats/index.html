The quick version

Cats are closing the gap on dogs. The 2026 AVMA Pet Ownership and Demographics Sourcebook counts 77 million pet cats in the U.S., up from 58.3 million in 2016, versus 88.9 million dogs. Among people who got a pet in the past two years, the share who picked a cat rose 6.1 points from 2025 to 2026, faster than the 4.1-point rise for dogs.

Why cats are trending

Smaller homes, busier schedules and lower costs all help. AVMA found average pet spending per household fell in 2026, and most owners see the vet once or twice a year, mostly for routine checkups. The CATalyst Council separately reported that cat clinic visits grew in the third quarter while overall visits fell.
