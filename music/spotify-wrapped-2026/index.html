📅 Why we expect Wednesday, December 2

Spotify keeps the date secret until launch morning, so nobody outside Spotify knows for sure. But the pattern is strong. Here's every launch since 2018:

Five Wednesdays in a row, and no launch later than December 4 in the past five years. Wednesday, December 2 fits both. One heads up if you've seen "December 3" floating around: that's a Thursday this year, which would break the Wednesday streak. Could Spotify surprise us? Totally. That's why the date above wears its EXPECTED badge until Spotify says otherwise.

⏱️ When does Spotify stop tracking for Wrapped?

Spotify hasn't confirmed the 2026 cutoff. For 2025, Spotify said Wrapped captured your listening from January 1 until mid November, so it had time to check the data before the December 3 launch. If 2026 works the same way, you've still got a few weeks to push that one artist into your Top 5. Sorry, the October 31 cutoff is a myth.

A couple of 2025 rules worth knowing: a song counts once you've played it for more than 30 seconds, offline listening counts, and white noise and nature sounds get filtered out. Private Mode sessions add to your total minutes but don't shape your taste-based stories.

📱 How to see your Spotify Wrapped

When it goes live, here's how it worked in 2025 (we'll update the steps if Spotify changes anything):

  1. Update the Spotify app. Make sure your Spotify mobile app (iOS or Android) is on the latest version.
  2. Open Home. Open the app and look for the Wrapped feed at the top of your Home screen.
  3. Or search for it. Search “2026 Wrapped” in the app if the banner doesn't show up.
  4. Tap through your stories. Tap through your personalized stories, then save or share the cards you love.

Wrapped was free for both Free and Premium users in nearly every market where Spotify is live. It's a mobile app experience, so open it on your phone.

Quick answers

When does Spotify Wrapped 2026 come out?

Spotify hasn't announced the date yet (checked October 19, 2026). Our best guess is Wednesday, December 2, 2026, because every Wrapped since 2021 has dropped on a Wednesday, and the last five landed between November 29 and December 4. Treat it as expected, not confirmed.

When does Spotify stop tracking for Wrapped 2026?

Spotify hasn't said for 2026. For 2025, Spotify said Wrapped captured listening from January 1 until mid November, a few weeks before the December 3 launch. So your November streams probably count, but the exact 2026 cutoff isn't confirmed.

Does Spotify Wrapped stop counting on October 31?

No, not in 2025. Spotify said the 2025 window ran to mid November. The October 31 cutoff is an old myth.

Why can't I see my Spotify Wrapped?

The most common reasons: it hasn't launched yet, your app needs an update, or you didn't meet the eligibility bar. In 2025 you needed at least 30 songs streamed for more than 30 seconds each and at least five different artists. See our Wrapped not showing fixes page for the full checklist.

Do Private Mode streams count toward Wrapped?

For 2025, Private Mode listening counted toward your total minutes, but not toward your taste-based stories or the eligibility minimum, per Spotify.

When is Apple Music Replay 2026 coming out?

Apple hasn't announced it. Apple Music Replay 2025 dropped December 2, 2025, and earlier editions landed between November 28 and December 3. Monthly Replay stats are already in the Music app all year.
