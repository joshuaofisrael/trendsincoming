Apple Music Replay 2026: How to Find Your Year in Music

By The Trends Incoming team · Published Oct 19, 2026 · Last updated Oct 19, 2026

Short answer: good news, you don't have to wait for all of it. Apple Music Replay now shows your top songs, artists and albums every month in the Music app. The big year-end Replay and highlight reel usually land in late November or early December EXPECTED, NOT CONFIRMED. Apple hasn't announced the 2026 date yet.

📱 How to see Apple Music Replay in the app

  1. Update your iPhone, iPad or Android to the latest OS.
  2. Open the Music app and go to Home.
  3. Scroll down to Replay: Your Top Music.
  4. Tap Go back in time to open your listening stats.

💻 How to see it on the web

  1. Go to replay.music.apple.com on any phone or computer.
  2. Sign in with the same Apple Account you use for Apple Music.
  3. Tap or click Jump In.

✨ What you get

  • Monthly insights: top songs, artists and albums by plays and time listened, plus milestones.
  • Year-end Replay: top songs, artists, albums, genres, playlists and stations at the end of the calendar year.
  • Highlight reel: an audio and visual recap you can share as a video.
  • Top listener badges: Apple tells you if you're in the top 100, 500 or 1,000 listeners of an artist or genre.
  • Your Replay playlist: updated weekly, plus an All Time playlist and past years.

🛠️ Replay not showing?

Apple's fix: turn on Use Listening History on every device. On iPhone or iPad it's Settings > Apps > Music. On a Mac, open Music > Settings > General. On Android, tap More > Settings in the Apple Music app. Once you've played enough music, Apple sends a notification that Replay is ready.

Quick answers

When does Apple Music Replay 2026 come out?

Apple hasn't announced a date. The year-end Replay dropped December 2, 2025, December 3, 2024, November 28, 2023 and November 29, 2022, so late November to early December is the safe bet. Monthly Replay stats are available in the Music app all year.

How do I see my Apple Music Replay?

In the Music app, go to Home, scroll to Replay: Your Top Music and tap Go back in time. On the web, go to replay.music.apple.com, sign in with the Apple Account you use for Apple Music and tap Jump In.

Why don't I see Apple Music Replay?

Apple says to make sure Use Listening History is turned on for all your devices. You'll get a notification once you've played enough music for Replay to be ready.

Do I need an Apple Music subscription for Replay?

Replay is built from your Apple Music listening history, and Apple says to sign in with the same Apple Account you use with your Apple Music subscription.
