🎟️ Presale steps

  1. Register now at ZachBryanPresale.com. Enter the two-factor code you get by text, or the sign-up won't stick, then look for the confirmation email.
  2. Get in before registration closes. The official FAQ says North America sign-ups close "Thursday, October 21 at 10:00pm EST / 7:00pm PST". October 21 is actually a Wednesday, and codes arrive the day before presale, so we'd treat Monday, Oct 19 as your real deadline and register today.
  3. Watch your email and texts on Tue Oct 20. Codes come the day before presale from AEG Presents, by email plus SMS for US numbers (Canada gets email only). Check spam too.
  4. Use the ticket link in that message. Each venue sells through its own official seller, which can be AXS, Ticketmaster or SeatGeek. Make sure you're signed in to that account before 10am.
  5. Be ready at 10am local on Wed Oct 21. Max 8 tickets per show per code, first come first served, and a code doesn't guarantee seats.
  6. Missed it? Try the local presale on Thu Oct 22 at 10am local (look for venue, radio or local partner emails), then the general on sale on Fri Oct 23 at 10am local at ZachBryan.com.

All North America sales are final, with no refunds or exchanges, per the official FAQ. Stick to official links, and be careful with resale listings posted before tickets even go on sale.

🧭 Plan the trip

Stadium shows clog roads and rideshare zones fast. Book a room early with free cancellation, check the stadium's own site for parking and bag rules closer to the date, and give yourself extra time to get in.
