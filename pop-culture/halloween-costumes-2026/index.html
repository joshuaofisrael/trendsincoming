The quick version

Halloween is Saturday, October 31, 2026, and there are two very different costume lists out there. The NRF survey tells you what people plan to wear (witches and vampires win, again). Google's Frightgeist tells you what's surging in searches (hello, Buddy the Unicorn). We put them side by side so you can tell a classic from a moment.

What the two lists tell you

If you want to blend in at the party, go NRF: witch, vampire, a superhero. If you want the "oh my gosh, I love that" reaction, go Frightgeist. The rest of Google's top 10 is Oliver Tree, Verity from Minecraft, Carl from Dungeon Crawler Carl, Disco Scooby-Doo and Hazel Hook from Descendants. Dolly Parton, who died in August, tops Google's kids list and is No. 2 for pets, and Michael Jackson's Thriller look is at a 10-year search high.

The pet report

NRF says the pumpkin (11.6%) and the hot dog (5.9%) are still the top pet costumes, with ghosts, bats and spiders climbing. Google's trending pet searches are pure chaos: a centipede, Dolly Parton, Bullseye from Toy Story and, yes, a can of Diet Coke.

Shopping tips

NRF says discount stores are the top place people shop for Halloween, followed by costume shops and online. The closer you get to the 31st, the thinner the racks, so the popular kids' sizes go first. Trick-or-treat hours are set locally, so check your town's announcement.

Quick answers

What is the most popular Halloween costume in 2026?

For adults, a witch, with about 4.8 million people planning to wear one, per NRF. For kids, Spider-Man, at about 4.6 million.

What is the top trending Halloween costume on Google in 2026?

Buddy the Unicorn tops Google's 2026 Frightgeist list of rising costume searches, followed by NeeDoh.

How much will Americans spend on Halloween in 2026?

NRF forecasts $13.5 billion, including $4.4 billion on costumes.

What day is Halloween 2026?

Saturday, October 31, 2026. Trick-or-treat hours are set locally.

What is the most popular pet costume for 2026?

A pumpkin, chosen by 11.6% of pet owners who plan to dress a pet, per NRF. A hot dog is second.
