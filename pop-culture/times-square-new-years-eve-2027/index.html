Times Square New Year's Eve 2027 in Person: Rules, Arrival Time and What to Bring

By The Trends Incoming team · Checked Oct 27, 2026

These rules come from the Times Square Alliance's New Year's Eve FAQ as posted for last year's celebration. The Alliance updates them in December, and we'll refresh this page when it does.

The basics

  • Free: no tickets needed and no public viewing spots are for sale. Beware of "all access" passes; the Alliance warns they don't get you past police lines.
  • Arrival: viewing areas opened at 3 p.m. last year, first come, first served.
  • Ball raise: around 6 p.m. ET. Ball drop: 60 second descent starting at 11:59 p.m. ET.
  • Best view: along Broadway from 43rd to 50th Street and along Seventh Avenue up to 59th Street.

How to get in

Enter from 6th Avenue or 8th Avenue only. Viewing area access points were at 45th, 49th, 52nd and 56th Streets, from 6th and 8th Avenues. You can't cross Broadway or Seventh Avenue once streets close. Take the subway, but skip the Times Square 42nd Street station on the night.

What you can't bring

Backpacks, large bags, umbrellas, lawn or folding chairs, picnic blankets, large coolers, alcohol, drones, weapons and other large items. All bags are searched. Public drinking is illegal in New York City.

The hard parts

  • No bathrooms: there are no portable restrooms.
  • No food vendors: no licensed food or drink vendors in the area.
  • No coming back: leave your spot, even to eat, and you can't return to it.
  • Cold: dress in layers for hours outside, possibly below freezing.

Alerts

NYC Emergency Management runs an opt in text service: text NEWYEARSEVE to 692692 for weather, closures and traffic (NOCHEVIEJA for Spanish).

Rather stay warm?

Watch on ABC or Disney+ from 8 p.m. ET. See how to watch Rockin' Eve 2027.

Quick answers

Do you need tickets for Times Square on New Year's Eve?

No. The Times Square Alliance says the outdoor celebration is free and first come, first served. No public viewing spaces are sold. Only private parties inside restaurants and hotels need tickets.

What time should I get to Times Square for New Year's Eve?

Last year the Alliance said viewing areas officially opened at 3 p.m., and people start arriving in the afternoon. Areas fill up north from 43rd Street, so earlier is better.

Are there bathrooms in Times Square on New Year's Eve?

No. The Alliance says there are no portable restrooms during the celebration, and if you leave your viewing area you can't return to your spot.

What can't you bring to Times Square on New Year's Eve?

Backpacks, large bags, umbrellas, folding chairs, picnic blankets, large coolers, alcohol, drones, weapons and other large items. All bags are searched.
