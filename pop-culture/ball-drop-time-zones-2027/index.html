Quick answers

What time does the ball drop in Central time?

11:00 p.m. Central on Thursday, December 31. The ball drops at midnight Eastern.

What time does the ball drop in Pacific time?

9:00 p.m. Pacific on Thursday, December 31.

What time does the ball drop in Mountain time?

10:00 p.m. Mountain on Thursday, December 31. Most of Arizona, which skips daylight saving time, is also on 10:00 p.m.

What time is midnight Eastern in Hawaii?

7:00 p.m. Hawaii time on December 31.
