The quick version

The 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade is Thursday, November 26, 2026, starting at 8:30 a.m. in all time zones. It airs live on NBC and Telemundo and streams on Peacock, Macy's says. The parade steps off at West 77th Street and Central Park West and ends at Macy's Herald Square around noon. Macy's is marking the centennial with a record 100 inflatable elements. How to watch the parade

  • On TV: NBC and Telemundo, from 8:30 a.m. in every time zone.
  • Streaming: Peacock carries it live. Check Peacock's current plan requirements before the day.
  • Without cable: an antenna that picks up your local NBC station, or a live TV streaming service that includes it. Check your local lineup.
  • Follow along: Macy's uses the hashtag #MacysParade.

The route, step by step

1Start at West 77th Street and Central Park West, beside the American Museum of Natural History

5West on 34th Street to Seventh Avenue, finishing at Macy's Herald Square around noon

Where to watch in person, and when it reaches you

Rockefeller Center's guide says the parade moves along Central Park West from about 8:30 to 10:30 a.m., and the lead units reach Sixth Avenue at 59th Street around 9:30 a.m. The farther south you stand, the later the first balloons arrive. Public viewing is on Central Park West from West 75th to West 61st streets (west side of the avenue only) and on Sixth Avenue from West 59th to West 38th streets. Not open to the public: the staging area at the start, Columbus Circle and Central Park South, Sixth Avenue from West 34th to West 38th streets, and 34th Street between Broadway and Seventh Avenue. The guide says early arrivals start around 6 a.m. and the route typically draws an estimated 3.5 million spectators.

Parade facts

The parade was first held in 1924. The count runs behind the calendar because it was canceled from 1942 through 1944 during World War II, per Rockefeller Center. The official page is macys.com/parade.

Quick answers

What time is the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade 2026?

It steps off at 8:30 a.m. on Thursday, November 26, 2026, from West 77th Street and Central Park West, and airs from 8:30 a.m. in all time zones. It ends at Macy's Herald Square around noon, per Rockefeller Center's guide.

What channel is the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade on?

Macy's says the 100th parade airs on NBC and Telemundo and streams on Peacock, starting at 8:30 a.m. in all time zones.

How can I watch the Macy's parade without cable?

Peacock streams it, and NBC airs it, so a TV antenna that gets your local NBC station works. Live TV streaming services that include your local NBC station should carry it too, so check your local channel lineup. Peacock's own plan requirements can change, so check Peacock before the day.

What new balloons are in the 100th Macy's parade?

Macy's confirms a record 100 inflatable elements, including a new Sorcerer's Apprentice Mickey, a reimagined Underdog (first flight since 1984), Billie Brightpaw, Tom Jr. and a returning Blue Hippopotamus. Announced separately: a new Bluey balloon (Oct 5), a Hello Kitty balloon (Oct 13) and Joe Cool with Woodstock (Sept 16).

Who is performing at the Macy's parade 2026?

We haven't found an official performer list yet. We'll add it here when NBC and Macy's announce it. Performers are typically announced closer to the parade.

What is the Macy's parade route?

South on Central Park West from 77th Street to Columbus Circle, east on Central Park South to Sixth Avenue, south on Sixth Avenue to 34th Street, then west on 34th Street to Seventh Avenue and Macy's Herald Square, about 2.5 miles.

Why is this the 100th parade if it started in 1924?

The count runs behind the calendar because the parade was canceled from 1942 through 1944 during World War II, per Rockefeller Center.
