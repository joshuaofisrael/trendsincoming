Medicare Part B Premium 2027: $209.40 a Month (Official CMS Numbers)

By The Trends Incoming team · Published Nov 18, 2026 · Official CMS sources only

Short answer: it's official. On November 17, 2026, the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) announced that the standard Medicare Part B premium for 2027 is $209.40 a month, up $6.50 from $202.90 in 2026. The annual Part B deductible rises to $292, up $9 from $283. Here's what that means for your Social Security check, and the full income-related premium table.

💰 How this affects your Social Security check

The Social Security Administration already announced a 3.6% cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) for 2027, adding about $75 a month to the average retirement benefit. Most people have their Part B premium deducted automatically from that same check. Because the Part B premium is rising about 3.2%, a smaller percentage than the 3.6% COLA, most beneficiaries should still see a net increase in their January 2027 payment, just smaller than the full COLA. CMS said the premium and deductible increase is "mainly due to projected increases in healthcare spending."

📈 2027 income-related monthly adjustment amount (IRMAA), official

About 9% of people with Part B pay more than the standard premium, based on modified adjusted gross income from two years prior (2025 income for 2027 premiums). These are the official CMS tiers for people with full Part B coverage:

Quick answers

What is the Medicare Part B premium for 2027?

$209.40 a month for most people, up $6.50 from $202.90 in 2026, per the CMS fact sheet released November 17, 2026.

What is the 2027 Medicare Part B deductible?

$292 a year, up $9 from $283 in 2026, per CMS.

Why is my Part B premium higher than $209.40?

About 9% of people with Part B pay more because of income, through the income-related monthly adjustment amount (IRMAA). It's based on your modified adjusted gross income from two years earlier (your 2025 tax return for 2027 premiums). See the table on this page for the exact tiers.

Does the Medicare Part B premium reduce my Social Security COLA?

Yes, for most beneficiaries. The Part B premium is usually deducted straight from your Social Security check before you get it. The 2027 COLA is 3.6%; the Part B premium is rising a smaller 3.2%, so most people still come out ahead, just not by the full COLA amount.

When do the 2027 Medicare premiums take effect?

January 1, 2027, for all Medicare Parts A and B premiums and deductibles.

Is this Medicare or tax advice for my situation?

No. This page explains the official 2027 numbers CMS published. For help choosing a plan or understanding your own premium, use Medicare.gov, call 1-800-MEDICARE, or talk to a licensed insurance counselor through your state's SHIP program.
