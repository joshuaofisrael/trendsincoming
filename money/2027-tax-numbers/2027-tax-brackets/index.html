🔍 How the 2027 projection works

Brackets and the standard deduction are indexed to inflation by law (Internal Revenue Code section 1(f)). The IRS compares the chained consumer price index (C-CPI-U) for the 12 months ending August 31 with a base year, then rounds each increase down to the next $50 ($25 for single and married filing separately brackets). Thomson Reuters and Wolters Kluwer ran that formula on BLS data from September 2025 through August 2026. One wrinkle: BLS never published an October 2025 CPI because of the 2025 government shutdown, so they used 11 months. The IRS could handle that month differently, which is exactly why these are labeled projected. Expect any differences to be small, usually a few hundred dollars at the thresholds.

🧾 Worked example (2026, official)

A single filer with $60,000 of taxable income in 2026 pays 10% on the first $12,400 ($1,240), 12% on the next $38,000 ($4,560) and 22% on the last $9,600 ($2,112). Total: $7,912 before credits. That matches the IRS table: $5,800 plus 22% of the amount over $50,400. Top bracket 22%, average rate about 13.2%.

Quick answers

What are the 2027 tax brackets?

The IRS has not published them yet (checked October 30, 2026). The seven rates stay 10%, 12%, 22%, 24%, 32%, 35% and 37% because they are permanent law. Projected 2027 thresholds from Thomson Reuters put the 10% bracket at up to $12,800 for single filers and $25,600 for joint filers, and the 37% rate above $661,375 single and $793,650 joint. These are estimates until the IRS release.

What is the standard deduction for 2027?

Not official yet. Projections from Thomson Reuters and Wolters Kluwer put it at $16,600 for single filers and married filing separately, $33,200 for married filing jointly and $24,900 for head of household. The official 2026 amounts are $16,100, $32,200 and $24,150.

When will the IRS announce the 2027 tax brackets?

The IRS 2026 to 2027 guidance plan lists the 2027 inflation revenue procedure for October 2026. Last year's came out October 9, 2025. The law requires the tables by December 15. We update this page the day the IRS posts them.

What are the 2027 tax brackets for married filing jointly?

Projected, not official: 10% up to $25,600; 12% to $104,050; 22% to $218,250; 24% to $416,650; 32% to $529,100; 35% to $793,650; 37% above that. The official 2026 joint brackets are in the table on this page.

Do the 2026 or 2027 brackets apply to the return I file in 2027?

The 2026 brackets. The IRS says the tax year 2026 amounts generally apply to returns filed in 2027. The 2027 brackets apply to returns filed in 2028.

Does moving into a higher bracket mean all my income is taxed at that rate?

No. Each rate applies only to the slice of taxable income inside its range. That's why your average rate is always lower than your top bracket.

When will the IRS release the 2027 tax brackets?

No date is announced. As of October 30, 2026, the newest IRS news release is IR-2026-129 (Oct 29). The 2026 numbers came out October 9, 2025, the 2025 numbers October 22, 2024, and the 2024 numbers November 9, 2023. The legal deadline is December 15.
