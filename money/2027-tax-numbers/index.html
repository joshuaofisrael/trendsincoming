🔍 How we label numbers

Official IRS means the figure is in an IRS revenue procedure, notice or news release, linked in the sources. Projected means a published estimate that applies the inflation formula in the tax code to government price data (we name whose estimate on each page). Projections can be off by a step or two of rounding, so don't treat them as final.

Quick answers

Are the 2027 tax brackets out?

Not yet. As of October 18, 2026 the IRS has not released them. Its guidance plan schedules them for October 2026, and we update the day they post.

Which 2027 tax numbers are already official?

The 2027 HSA limits: $4,500 self-only and $9,000 family (Rev. Proc. 2026-24). Brackets, the standard deduction, 401(k) and IRA limits and the mileage rate are still pending.

When will each 2027 IRS number come out?

Brackets and standard deduction: October 2026. 401(k), IRA and other retirement limits: November 2026. Standard mileage rate: December 2026. HSA: already out (spring 2026).

Is this tax advice?

No. This hub is general information built from IRS releases, with projections clearly labeled. For your own situation, use IRS.gov or a qualified tax professional.
