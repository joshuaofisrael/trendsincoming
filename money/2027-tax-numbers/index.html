Quick answers

Are the 2027 tax brackets out?

Yes, since November 5, 2026 (IRS IR-2026-133 and Rev. Proc. 2026-35).

Which 2027 tax numbers are already official?

Brackets and standard deduction, 401(k) and IRA limits, HSA limits, gift and estate tax, Social Security COLA, Medicare Part B premium, the Part D out-of-pocket threshold and standard deductible, and most January 1 state minimum wages are official. Only the IRS mileage rate is still pending, expected in December 2026.

When did each 2027 IRS number come out?

HSA limits: spring 2026 (Rev. Proc. 2026-24). Brackets, standard deduction (including the age and blindness add-ons) and gift/estate tax: November 5, 2026 (IR-2026-133 and Rev. Proc. 2026-35). 401(k), IRA and other retirement limits: November 12, 2026 (IR-2026-136). Mileage rate: still pending as of November 21, 2026. The Part D cap is a CMS figure, not an IRS one: April 6, 2026 rate announcement.
