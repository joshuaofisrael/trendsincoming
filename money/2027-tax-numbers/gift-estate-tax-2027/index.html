Gift and Estate Tax Numbers for 2027 (Official IRS Figures)

By The Trends Incoming team · Published Nov 18, 2026 · Last updated Nov 18, 2026

Short answer: the IRS announced the 2027 gift and estate tax numbers on November 5, 2026, alongside the rest of the 2027 inflation adjustments, in news release IR-2026-133 and Revenue Procedure 2026-35. The annual gift tax exclusion rises to $20,000 per recipient, and the estate tax basic exclusion amount rises to $15,480,000 per person.

🔍 What changed and why

The annual gift exclusion and the estate tax basic exclusion amount are both indexed to inflation under the Internal Revenue Code. The One Big Beautiful Bill Act raised the estate and gift tax basic exclusion amount to $15,000,000 starting in 2026, and it's indexed for inflation each year after that. The 2027 figure of $15,480,000 is the first inflation adjustment under that higher base.

🧮 What this means in practice

In 2027, you can give up to $20,000 to any number of people without filing IRS Form 709 or using any of your lifetime exemption. A married couple can combine their annual exclusions to give $40,000 per recipient. For estates, the $15,480,000 exemption is the amount you can pass on, during life or at death, before federal estate or gift tax applies; with portability, a surviving spouse can generally use any exemption their late spouse didn't use, for a combined shield of roughly $30.96 million.

Also new for 2027: the tax brackets and standard deduction and 401(k) and IRA limits are official too.

Quick answers

What is the gift tax exclusion for 2027?

$20,000 per recipient, up from $19,000 in 2026, per IRS news release IR-2026-133 (Nov 5, 2026) and Rev. Proc. 2026-35. You can give that amount to as many people as you like in 2027 without filing a gift tax return or touching your lifetime exclusion.

What is the estate tax exemption for 2027?

$15,480,000 per person, up from $15,000,000 for people who died in 2026, per IR-2026-133. A married couple can generally shield about $30.96 million combined with portability.

What is the gift tax exclusion for a non-citizen spouse in 2027?

$201,000, up from $194,000 in 2026, per IR-2026-133. This separate, larger exclusion applies only to gifts to a spouse who is not a US citizen.

Is the generation-skipping transfer tax exemption the same as the estate tax exemption?

Yes. IRS Rev. Proc. 2026-35 says the $15,480,000 basic exclusion amount for 2027 is also used to set the generation-skipping transfer exemption under Internal Revenue Code section 2631(c).

Do most people owe federal estate or gift tax?

No. With a $15,480,000 per-person exemption in 2027, only very large estates and lifetime gifts are affected. This page is general information, not tax or legal advice; talk to a qualified estate planning attorney or tax professional about your own situation.
