HSA Limits 2027: $4,500 Self-Only, $9,000 Family (Official)

By The Trends Incoming team · Published Oct 18, 2026 · Last updated Oct 18, 2026

Short answer: this one's locked in. IRS Revenue Procedure 2026-24 sets the 2027 health savings account limit at $4,500 for self-only coverage and $9,000 for family coverage. That's $100 and $250 more than 2026. If you're picking a plan in open enrollment right now, these are the real numbers.

Why it's in the news now

Open season is here. Medicare open enrollment runs through December 7, and HealthCare.gov open enrollment starts November 1, with most workplace benefits windows in between. People comparing a high-deductible plan want the 2027 HSA math, and unlike the brackets, it's already official. One heads up: once you're enrolled in Medicare, you generally can't make new HSA contributions. IRS Publication 969 has the rules.

Quick answers

What is the HSA limit for 2027?

Official: $4,500 for self-only coverage and $9,000 for family coverage, set by IRS Revenue Procedure 2026-24. That's up from $4,400 and $8,750 in 2026.

What is the HSA catch-up contribution for 2027?

$1,000 extra if you're 55 or older. That amount is fixed in the law and isn't indexed for inflation.

What counts as a high-deductible health plan in 2027?

For 2027, the deductible must be at least $1,750 for self-only or $3,500 for family coverage, and out-of-pocket costs (not premiums) can't exceed $8,700 self-only or $17,400 family.

Are the 2027 HSA limits official?

Yes. Unlike brackets and 401(k) limits, the IRS publishes HSA amounts in the spring. Rev. Proc. 2026-24 is on IRS.gov.
