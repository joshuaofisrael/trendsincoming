FAFSA 2027-28: Open Date and Every Deadline That Matters

By The Trends Incoming team · Published 2026-11-19 · Last updated 2026-11-19

Short answer: the 2027-28 FAFSA is open now at fafsa.gov. The Department of Education launched it the week of September 23 to 24, 2026, the second year in a row it beat the October 1 floor. The federal deadline is June 30, 2028. State and school deadlines are often much earlier, so the federal date is usually the wrong one to plan around.

Federal deadline, in the form's own words

The official 2027-28 FAFSA PDF says: for federal aid, submit as early as possible, but no earlier than October 1, 2026, and Federal Student Aid must receive the application no later than June 30, 2028. Your college must have your correct, complete information by your last day of enrollment in the 2027-28 school year.

Online filing at fafsa.gov is what FSA recommends if you are close to a deadline.

School deadlines

Colleges often set priority filing dates for institutional aid that are earlier than both the federal and state dates. The form tells applicants to check with a high school counselor or the financial aid office at each college listed. List every school you are considering on the FAFSA so those offices can pull your data.

What changed this cycle

ED's Homeroom post (Sept 24, 2026) highlights real-time Student Aid Index and Pell Grant results for most filers, reusable parent information across siblings, a QR-code contributor invite, and clearer instructions. None of that changes the deadline math: early filing still wins for limited state and campus funds.

Quick answers

When did the 2027-28 FAFSA open?

The Department of Education made the 2027-28 FAFSA available the week of September 23 to 24, 2026. The Homeroom blog dated September 24, 2026 says the form is available nationwide at fafsa.gov. That beat the October 1 statutory floor.

What is the federal FAFSA deadline for 2027-28?

Federal Student Aid must receive your application no later than June 30, 2028. Your college must also have your correct, complete information by your last day of enrollment in the 2027-28 school year. The printed form still says not to submit earlier than October 1, 2026.

Why file before the federal deadline?

State and college aid often run out earlier, and some awards are first-come, first-served. The official form says state or college deadlines may be as early as October 1, 2026.

Where do I find my state's FAFSA deadline?

Page 2 of the official 2027-28 FAFSA PDF lists state deadlines. A short sample is on this page; always confirm on the PDF or with your state agency.

Is this financial-aid advice?

No. We restate official dates and link the primary sources. Talk to your school's financial aid office about your own situation.
