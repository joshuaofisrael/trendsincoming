New Jersey Minimum Wage 2027: $16.48 an Hour

By The Trends Incoming team · Published Oct 19, 2026 · Last updated Oct 19, 2026

New Jersey's minimum wage rises 56 cents to $16.48 an hour for most workers on January 1, 2027. Small and seasonal employers move to $16.25, and farm workers finally hit the $15 benchmark. NJDOL announced the new rates on September 29, 2026.

📅 How it's set

New Jersey's constitution ties the minimum wage to the Consumer Price Index, so it's adjusted every January 1. Small and seasonal employers finish their catch-up schedule in 2028, and farm employers in 2030; after that they rise with inflation too.

Quick answers

What is New Jersey's minimum wage in 2027?

$16.48 an hour for most employees from January 1, 2027, per the New Jersey Department of Labor and Workforce Development.

What is the New Jersey minimum wage for small businesses in 2027?

$16.25 an hour for employers with fewer than six employees, and for seasonal employees.

What is the tipped minimum wage in New Jersey in 2027?

A $6.61 cash wage, with a maximum tip credit of $9.87. If tips don't make up the difference, the employer pays it.

What is the farm worker minimum wage in New Jersey in 2027?

$15.00 an hour for farm workers paid hourly or by piece rate.
