California Minimum Wage 2027: $17.40 an Hour

By The Trends Incoming team · Published Oct 19, 2026 · Last updated Oct 19, 2026

California's statewide minimum wage goes from $16.90 to $17.40 an hour on January 1, 2027. That's 50 cents more, or about $20 a week and $1,040 a year for a full-time worker. The Department of Finance certified it on July 31, 2026, and it applies to every employer size.

Quick answers

What is California's minimum wage in 2027?

$17.40 an hour from January 1, 2027, for all employers, unless a higher local or industry rate applies.

How is California's minimum wage set?

It rises each year with the CPI-W inflation index, capped at 3.5% a year, and it can't go down. The Department of Finance certified the 2027 rate on July 31, 2026.

What is the exempt salary threshold in California for 2027?

$72,384 a year, which is twice the state minimum wage for full-time work. Exempt employees also have to meet the duties tests.

Can California employers count tips toward minimum wage?

No. California doesn't allow a tip credit.
