🍽️ Tipped workers

In many states the tipped cash wage moves with the minimum, and tips have to make up the rest. If cash wage plus tips falls short, the employer pays the difference. A few 2027 numbers straight from the states: Colorado $12.69, Arizona $12.65, Maine $7.85, New Jersey $6.61, Michigan $6.30 (42% of the minimum), South Dakota $6.15 and Ohio $5.70. California and Washington don't allow a tip credit at all, so tipped workers get the full minimum.

⏳ The pending one: Vermont

Vermont ties its minimum wage to inflation and raises it every January 1, but it hadn't posted the 2027 number when we checked on October 19, 2026 (today it's $14.42). Bloomberg Tax's calculation points to about $14.91; we'll swap in the official rate the day Vermont posts it.

Heads up on early reports: some trackers still list Arizona as "expected." It's final. The Industrial Commission of Arizona posted its 2027 poster on September 25, 2026: $15.65.

🗽 Not going up on January 1

New York skips its 2027 inflation raise because an off-ramp in state law was triggered, so it stays $17.00 downstate and $16.00 upstate. The federal $7.25 hasn't changed either. Several other states adjust on different dates, so check your state labor department if yours isn't listed.

Quick answers

Which states raise the minimum wage on January 1, 2027?

Sixteen states: Washington, Connecticut, California, Rhode Island, New Jersey, Colorado, Maine, Arizona, Nebraska, Michigan, Vermont, Virginia, South Dakota, Minnesota, Ohio and Montana. Fifteen have a confirmed rate. Vermont hasn't posted its 2027 rate yet (checked October 19, 2026).

What is the highest state minimum wage in 2027?

Washington, at $17.73 an hour from January 1, 2027. Connecticut ($17.48) and California ($17.40) are next. Some cities and Washington, D.C. are higher.

Which state has the biggest minimum wage raise in 2027?

Michigan, up $1.27 from $13.73 to $15.00 an hour. Rhode Island goes up $1.00 to $17.00, and Virginia goes up $0.98 to $13.75.

Is New York's minimum wage going up in 2027?

No. The New York State Department of Labor says it stays $17.00 in New York City, Long Island and Westchester and $16.00 in the rest of the state on January 1, 2027, because an inflation off-ramp in state law was triggered. Home care aides are the exception.

Is the federal minimum wage going up in 2027?

No. The federal minimum wage is $7.25 an hour and no change is scheduled.

Is Arizona's 2027 minimum wage final?

Yes. The Industrial Commission of Arizona posted its 2027 minimum wage poster on September 25, 2026, showing $15.65 an hour, with a $12.65 tipped cash wage.

If my city has a higher minimum wage, which one applies?

Generally the highest one that covers you: federal, state or local. This page lists statewide rates. Check your city or county, especially in California, Washington, Colorado and Maine.

Is this legal advice?

No. It's general information from official state sources. Confirm your situation with your state labor department or a qualified professional.
