Michigan Minimum Wage 2027: $15.00 an Hour

By The Trends Incoming team · Published Oct 19, 2026 · Last updated Oct 19, 2026

Michigan gets the biggest raise in the country: the minimum wage jumps from $13.73 to $15.00 an hour on January 1, 2027. That's $1.27 more an hour, about $50.80 a week and $2,642 a year for a full-time worker. It's written into state law, so it's locked in.

The tipped share keeps climbing: 44% of the minimum in 2028, 46% in 2029, 48% in 2030 and 50% from 2031. Tips plus cash wage must reach $15.00, or the employer makes up the difference.

Overtime rules don't change: time and a half after 40 hours in a workweek.

Quick answers

What is Michigan's minimum wage in 2027?

$15.00 an hour from January 1, 2027, under state law, per Michigan's Department of Labor and Economic Opportunity.

What is the tipped minimum wage in Michigan in 2027?

$6.30 an hour, 42% of the minimum, as long as tips bring total pay to at least $15.00.

What is the minimum wage for minors in Michigan in 2027?

Workers under 18 can be paid 85% of the minimum, which is $12.75 an hour.

How much more will a full-time Michigan minimum wage worker make in 2027?

About $50.80 a week, or about $2,642 a year before taxes, at 40 hours a week.
