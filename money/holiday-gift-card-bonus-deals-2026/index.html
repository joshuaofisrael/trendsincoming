Best deal for your own dining

  1. Lou Malnati's, $25 per $100. Best if you eat Chicago deep dish anyway. Buy from Nov 16, use the bonus Jan 2 to Feb 28, 2027.
  2. Atlas Restaurant Group, $25 per $100. The longest window: bonus eGift cards last until June 30, 2027. Online eGift cards only.
  3. Applebee's, $20 per $100. The widest reach and the longest buy window, through January 3, 2027.
  4. Landry's, $20 per $100. One reward card works across many brands, but it needs a minimum food purchase, skips Feb 12 to 14, and caps the promotion at $500 per household.

Bonus card rules to know

  • The bonus card is separate from the gift card. It has its own start date, end date and limits.
  • Most bonus cards do not work in December. Put the first and last usable day in your calendar.
  • Many require a minimum food purchase and allow one card per table or visit.
  • Online purchases usually email the bonus to the buyer, not the gift recipient.

Quick answers

What is the best restaurant gift card bonus for 2026?

On the offers we confirmed, Lou Malnati's and Atlas Restaurant Group give the most: a $25 bonus for every $100 in gift cards, worth $25 per $100. Applebee's and the Landry's restaurants give $20 per $100.

What is the Applebee's gift card deal for 2026?

Applebee's gives a $10 bonus card when you buy $50 in gift cards, online or in participating restaurants, from October 12, 2026 to January 3, 2027, according to its holiday gift card page. The bonus card has its own expiry and restrictions printed on the card.

When can I use my bonus card?

Most bonus cards do not work in December. Landry's reward cards work January 1 to March 31, 2027, except February 12 to 14. Lou Malnati's bonus cards work January 2 to February 28, 2027. Atlas bonus eGift cards work from your next visit until June 30, 2027.

Is a bonus card the same as a gift card?

No. The gift card you buy keeps its value, but the bonus or reward card is a promotional card with its own dates, minimum spend and blackout days. If it expires unused, it is worth nothing.
