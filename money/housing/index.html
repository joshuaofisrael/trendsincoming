What moves mortgage rates

Mortgage rates tend to follow the 10-year Treasury yield, which has been sitting above 5% as inflation worries linger and oil stays expensive. When the yield falls, mortgage rates usually ease a few days later. Freddie Mac's number is a weekly average, so your quote depends on your credit, down payment and loan type.

Shopping this fall? Compare official Loan Estimates from several lenders on the same day, and ask about points and fees, not just the rate.
