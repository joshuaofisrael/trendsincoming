Open enrollment: don't sleep on these dates

By The Trends Incoming team

Nov 1, 2026Open enrollment starts on HealthCare.gov
Dec 15, 2026Deadline for coverage that starts Jan 1
Jan 15, 2027Open enrollment ends
Feb 1, 2027Coverage starts if you signed up Dec 16 to Jan 15

Real talk: Dec 15 is the date that matters for most people. Miss Jan 15 and you'll need a qualifying life event (moving, losing coverage, a new baby) to get in. Some states run their own marketplaces with different deadlines, so check yours. Medicaid and CHIP take applications all year.

Picking a high-deductible plan? The 2027 HSA limits are official at $4,500 self-only and $9,000 family. More in our 2027 tax numbers tracker.
