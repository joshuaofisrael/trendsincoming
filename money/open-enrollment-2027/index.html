Open enrollment 2027: the dates that matter

Nov 1, 2026Open enrollment begins on HealthCare.gov for 2027 coverage
Dec 15, 2026Last day to enroll or change plans for coverage starting Jan 1
Jan 1, 2027Coverage starts if you enrolled by Dec 15 and paid your first premium
Jan 15, 2027Open enrollment ends
Feb 1, 2027Coverage starts for enrollments Dec 16 to Jan 15

States that run their own marketplaces may set different deadlines, so check your state's exchange. Outside open enrollment you need a Special Enrollment Period (for example, moving, losing coverage, marriage or a new baby). Medicaid and CHIP accept applications all year.
