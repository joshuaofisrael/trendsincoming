Quick answers

How much is the 2027 Social Security COLA?

3.6%, announced by SSA on October 14, 2026. The average retirement benefit rises about $75 a month.

When does the 2027 COLA start?

SSI with the December 31, 2026 payment; Social Security with January 2027 payments on January 13, 20 or 27, or December 31 for the 3rd-of-month group.

What is the 401(k) limit for 2027?

Not official yet. It's projected at $25,500, up from the official 2026 limit of $24,500. The IRS notice is due in November.
