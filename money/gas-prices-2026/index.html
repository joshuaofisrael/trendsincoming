Why it matters beyond the pump

Diesel moves trucks, so it feeds into shipping and grocery costs. If you drive for work, the IRS raised its business mileage rate to 76 cents a mile from July 1, 2026, citing fuel prices.

Read why oil hit $110, the Strait of Hormuz explainer and IRS mileage rates.

Quick answers

Why are gas prices so high right now?

Oil jumped as ships were attacked near the Strait of Hormuz, Houthi missiles targeted Saudi Arabia and strikes on Russian refineries resumed. Brent touched $110 on October 16, 2026.

What is the national average for gas?

About $4.33 a gallon for regular on October 16, 2026, per AAA. Diesel was $6.33.

Where can I check gas prices near me?

AAA's gas prices site has state and metro averages, and the EIA publishes official weekly prices.
