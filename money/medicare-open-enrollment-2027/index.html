The quick version

Medicare Open Enrollment runs October 15 to December 7, 2026, for coverage that starts January 1, 2027. It's the yearly window to switch Medicare Advantage or Part D plans, and it's separate from HealthCare.gov, which opens November 1.

Quick answers

When is Medicare Open Enrollment for 2027?

October 15 to December 7, 2026. Changes take effect January 1, 2027.

Is Medicare Open Enrollment the same as HealthCare.gov open enrollment?

No. HealthCare.gov Marketplace enrollment is a separate program and runs November 1, 2026 to January 15, 2027.

Where do I compare Medicare plans?

Medicare.gov Plan Finder, or call 1-800-MEDICARE.

What is the 2027 Medicare Part B premium?

Not announced yet. SSA says it will show in my Social Security accounts from late November.
