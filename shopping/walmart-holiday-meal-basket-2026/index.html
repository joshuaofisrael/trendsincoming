The quick version

Walmart's 2026 Holiday Meal Basket serves 10 people for $35, or $3.50 a person, Walmart announced on October 28. It has 26 items, 18% more than last year, and it's available in stores and online through December 24, 2026. That makes it the lowest total in our Thanksgiving dinner deals comparison, just under Lidl's $35.86.

What's in the basket

Walmart lists a Butterball turkey, Stove Top stuffing (2 boxes), fresh cranberries, a 5 lb bag of russet potatoes, 3 sweet potatoes, brown gravy mix, 3 cans of green beans, Campbell's cream of mushroom soup, French fried onions, 3 cans of corn, 2 boxes of JIFFY corn muffin mix, Jet-Puffed mini marshmallows, brown and serve rolls, pumpkin, evaporated milk, pie crusts and whipped topping, per its Oct 28 announcement.

The fine print

The $35 is an estimate: Walmart says it's based on a 13.5 lb turkey at 97 cents a pound and 3.2 lb of sweet potatoes at 88 cents a pound, so a bigger bird costs more. Delivery or pickup fees may apply, availability varies, and the basket excludes AK, HI, OK, PR and WI.
