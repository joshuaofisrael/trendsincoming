Thanksgiving and Black Friday 2026: which stores are open

Walmart, Costco, Home Depot and Lowe's close on Thanksgiving, Nov 26, and Target has said it does too. Black Friday hours are mostly not announced yet; we show last year's times and update as stores confirm.

The quick version

Most big retailers are closed on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, November 26, 2026, and open again on Black Friday, November 27. Walmart said on October 7 that its stores will be closed on Thanksgiving. Costco, Home Depot and Lowe's are closed too, and Target has said since 2021 that it closes. Most chains have not posted 2026 Black Friday hours yet. Grocery stores and pharmacies

Grocery stores and pharmacies are the main exception to Thanksgiving closures. FreshCalendars says many open Thanksgiving morning with shorter hours. We haven't confirmed chain-by-chain 2026 hours yet, so check your store's page or app, and don't leave your Thanksgiving shopping until the last minute.

Quick answers

What stores are open on Thanksgiving Day 2026?

Most big-box retailers are closed on Thursday, November 26, 2026. Walmart says its stores will be closed on Thanksgiving, Costco lists Thanksgiving Day among its U.S. warehouse closures, and Home Depot and Lowe's are closed too. Target has said since 2021 that its stores close on Thanksgiving.

Is Walmart open on Thanksgiving 2026?

No. In its Oct 7, 2026 holiday post, Walmart said: "Stores will be closed again on Thanksgiving and Christmas this year."

Is Costco open on Thanksgiving?

No. Costco's customer service page lists Thanksgiving Day among the days its U.S. warehouses are closed.

What time do stores open on Black Friday 2026?

Home Depot says it opens at 6 a.m. on Friday, November 27. Most other big chains had not posted 2026 hours as of our last check, so the table shows last year's times and says so. Hours vary by location.

Is Target open on Thanksgiving 2026?

Target said in 2021 that its stores would close on Thanksgiving Day going forward, per the Tenmomo tracker. Target has not announced 2026 Black Friday hours that we can find; it opened at 6 a.m. in 2025.

Are grocery stores and pharmacies open on Thanksgiving?

Many open Thanksgiving morning with shorter hours, FreshCalendars says, but hours vary by chain and location. Check your store's page or app.

When will Black Friday 2026 hours be announced?

Retailers usually post them in the weeks before. Tenmomo's tracker showed 1 of 64 retailers had posted 2026 Black Friday hours as of Oct 20. We update this page as stores announce.
