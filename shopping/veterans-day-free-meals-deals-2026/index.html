What ID counts as proof of service?

Each brand sets its own rules, and most staff just need to see something that shows you served. These are the common forms, and the table above shows what each brand says it accepts:

  • Military ID: the Uniformed Services ID card for active duty, Guard, Reserve and retirees. Chipotle and Red Robin both name it.
  • VA ID: the Veteran Health Identification Card or a VA-issued Veteran ID Card. Chipotle accepts both.
  • State ID or driver's license with a Veteran designation: most states can add one. Chipotle accepts it.
  • Discharge paperwork (DD 214): Chipotle accepts discharge paperwork. Bring a copy, not your only original.
  • ID.me: Pilot verifies veterans and service members through ID.me inside its app, so set that up before the day.

Not sure what your local restaurant will take? Call ahead. Brands that say "proof of service" without a list may still turn away some forms.

Post office, banks and closures on Nov 11

Post offices: USPS lists Veterans Day, Wednesday, November 11, 2026, among its 2026 holidays, so Post Offices are closed and regular mail resumes Thursday, November 12 (USPS holiday list). Banks: the Federal Reserve observes Veterans Day on November 11, 2026 (Federal Reserve holiday schedule). Many banks follow the Fed calendar, but not all, so check your branch. Retail stores and restaurants are generally open.

Quick answers

What restaurants give free meals on Veterans Day 2026?

As of Oct 25, 2026, we have confirmed 5 offers on the brands' own sites: Chipotle, Red Robin, Chili's, Golden Corral, Pilot and Flying J. Most are dine-in only at participating locations on Wednesday, November 11, 2026. We add more only after the brand posts its 2026 offer.

Is the post office open on Veterans Day 2026?

No. The U.S. Postal Service lists Wednesday, November 11, 2026 (Veterans Day) as one of its 2026 holidays, and Post Offices close on postal holidays. Regular mail and retail service resume the next day. Self-service kiosks in some lobbies and usps.com stay available.

Are banks open on Veterans Day 2026?

The Federal Reserve lists Veterans Day, November 11, 2026, as a holiday it observes, so Federal Reserve Banks are closed and most transfers that run through them won't settle that day. Many banks follow the Fed and close branches, but some stay open, so check your own bank.

Is Veterans Day a federal holiday in 2026?

Yes. Veterans Day is Wednesday, November 11, 2026. Both the Federal Reserve and USPS list it as an observed holiday for 2026.

What ID do veterans need for free meals?

It depends on the brand, so check the table. Chipotle accepts a valid military ID, a VA-issued veteran ID or health ID, a state ID with a Veteran designation, or discharge paperwork. Red Robin asks for a valid military ID or proof of service. Pilot verifies through ID.me in its app.

Do I have to dine in to get the free meal?

Usually yes. Chipotle, Red Robin, Chili's and Golden Corral all say their offers are in-restaurant or dine-in only. Pilot's offer is claimed in the Pilot app.

Can my family eat free too?

Generally no. The offers are for the veteran or service member. Golden Corral says family and friends are welcome but pay regular prices.
