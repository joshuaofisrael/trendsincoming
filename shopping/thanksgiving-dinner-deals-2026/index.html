Quick answers

What is the cheapest Thanksgiving dinner deal in 2026?

As of Oct 25, 2026, Lidl's basket is the lowest total we've verified at $35.86 for up to 10 people ($3.59 each), but it needs a Lidl Plus account and runs Nov 11 to 25. Aldi's meal for 10 is under $39 with no membership. Target's $30 meal serves 6, or $5 a person.

Where is the cheapest turkey in 2026?

Target says its Good & Gather frozen turkey returns at 79 cents per pound from November, wherever it's sold. Aldi and Lidl include the turkey in their meal price rather than listing a per-pound price.

When do Thanksgiving 2026 deals start?

Aldi: most items from Oct 14, cranberries Oct 26, turkey Nov 4, pie mix Nov 11, all through Nov 25. Sam's Club: orderable from Oct 28. Target: Nov 8 to 29. Lidl: Nov 11 to 25.

Has Walmart announced its 2026 Thanksgiving meal?

Not as of Oct 25, 2026. Walmart's Oct 7 holiday announcement doesn't include a meal basket. We'll add it as soon as Walmart posts it.

Do I need a membership for these deals?

Aldi says no coupons, loyalty cards or memberships. Lidl's price is for Lidl Plus members only. Sam's Club needs a club membership. Target's meal has no membership requirement in its announcement.

What day is Thanksgiving 2026?

Thursday, November 26, 2026.
