The quick version

Two big recalls are on drivers' radar this month: Ford's 4.38 million vehicle recall for trailer brake controls on F-Series, Ranger, Maverick and some Lincoln models, and about 202,000 Jeep and Wagoneer SUVs whose tire-pressure monitors may not warn you. The fastest way to know if yours is affected is a free VIN check at NHTSA.

Where's my VIN?Driver-side dashboard by the windshield, the door jamb sticker, or your registration

How to check in two minutes

  1. Find your 17-character VIN.
  2. Enter it at NHTSA's recall lookup.
  3. If there's an open recall, call a dealer to book the free fix. Parts can take time, so ask about interim safety steps.

Quick answers

How do I check if my car has a recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It's free.

Do recall repairs cost money?

No. Safety recall repairs are done free at authorized dealers.

Which Fords are in the trailer brake recall?

F-Series, Ranger, Maverick and some Lincoln models, about 4.38 million vehicles in all. Check your VIN to be sure.
